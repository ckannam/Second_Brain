## Working title
**How AI Is Lowering the Barriers to Entrepreneurship in [Country]'s Emerging Economy**

## The question
For most of history, founding a company demanded three scarce resources: **capital** to build before you earn, **expertise** across engineering, design, law, and market research, and **infrastructure** to reach customers. In economies where those resources were hardest to assemble, most would-be founders simply couldn't start. Artificial intelligence is now collapsing all three barriers at once — a single founder can prototype software, draft contracts, run market research, and reach customers with tools that cost almost nothing. **My project asks where, and for whom, that unlock matters most — and how it is reshaping who gets to become an entrepreneur in [Country].**

## Why [Country], why [Host]
[Country] is one of the clearest places in the world to study this: [1–2 sentences tailored to the host — e.g. "the Northeast of Brazil, anchored by Porto Digital and CIn-UFPE in Recife, is building a fast-growing digital economy outside the traditional São Paulo–Rio axis"]. Affiliating with **[Host]** would give me a front-row view of founders actually using AI to build, plus the scholarly framing to make sense of it.

## Approach (9 months)
1. **Embed** with [Host]'s founders, incubatees, and faculty to observe how early-stage ventures use AI day to day.
2. **Interview** a cross-section of founders, investors, and support-org leaders on which barrier — capital, expertise, or infrastructure — AI relaxes most in this ecosystem, and what new bottlenecks appear.
3. **Case studies** of ventures that could not plausibly have started five years ago, tracing exactly which AI capabilities made them viable.
4. **Synthesize** findings into a written analysis (and share back with the host community) on how AI is widening — or unevenly distributing — access to entrepreneurship.

## Why me
I work at **Johns Hopkins Technology Ventures** on the venture-capital and translational-funding team, where I map how early-stage technologies find their path to funding — so I bring a practitioner's view of how startups actually get resourced and de-risked. I also **co-own a student logistics venture**, giving me firsthand experience of the barriers this project studies. My aim is not to extract data but to contribute to [Host]'s community while learning from it.

## Deliverable & engagement
A rigorous, publishable write-up plus presentations to the host community; ongoing collaboration with [Host] researchers/founders throughout the grant.
