another country?* That's exactly the question you can answer better than any of my other recommenders.

## Experience 1 — The VC-matching "Second Brain" tool (my flagship)
This is the clearest proof of the whole thesis. I built an internal tool where anyone on the team
can type in a VC firm and instantly get back the Hopkins technologies that firm is most likely to
fund — with a fit score, warm-intro paths through JHU alumni, and a ready-to-send one-pager (and it
runs in reverse: technology → best-fit investors). I built it end-to-end with AI (Claude Code),
including a scoring rubric and a self-extending research backend that goes and profiles a new firm
the tool has never seen.

**What it's worth emphasizing (whatever you personally observed):**
- I took work that used to require expensive expertise and manual hours and **compressed it with AI
  into something a single person can run** — which *is* the phenomenon my Fulbright studies. I'm not
  an outside observer of "AI lowering barriers"; I'm a case study of it.
- I **self-directed** it — scoped the problem, made the engineering tradeoffs, and shipped and
  maintained a real tool the team uses, without needing it handed to me. (Feasibility abroad is
  really a question of self-direction, and this is the cleanest evidence of it.)
- I can **translate between the science, the capital, and the technology** — the exact bridge the
  project sits on.
