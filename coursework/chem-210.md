## Grading
- **Exams dominate.** Better of {midterm average, final} = **50%**; the lower of the two = **35%**. Discussions + Mastering Chemistry HW = **15%**.
- **Discussions:** 5 pts each (on-time + participate + don't leave early). **HW:** 10 pts each (Mastering Chemistry); 93% earns full credit, scaled generously below.
- **Resurrection quizzes:** after each midterm, correct your exam → quiz in discussion regains up to **25%** of lost points (more if the class average was <65%). Formula:
  `Exam Score = Student Score + (100 − Student Score) × (Points Earned / Points Possible) × 0.25`
- **Final grade scale:** 80-70-60-50 for A–D; top/bottom 25% of each band = ±. (So 85–94 = A, 95–100 = A+.)
- **AI policy:** the Mastering Chemistry embedded AI tutor is OK as a study aid; using *any* LLM to answer assigned HW is discouraged, and AI answers to in-class Learning Catalytics questions can be a **Duke Community Standard violation**.
