# NEUROSCI 206 — Human Brain Anatomy & Foundations (Week 1)

Week 1 knowledge note for [[neurosci-206]] ("Introducing Systems Neuroscience and the Human Brain"). Built from the
[[neurosci-206-first-class-human-brain|first-class deck]] + syllabus. **Reading:** *Neuroscience* 7e — **Appendix:
Survey of Human Neuroanatomy**. This is the anatomical vocabulary the whole course (sensory + motor systems) is built on.

## What "systems neuroscience" is
The study of the **structure and function of neural circuits** that interact across levels of the nervous system to
**produce behavior**. Circuits are organized into **systems** that process one modality (vision, touch, taste) or
integrate across them (motor control, attention), usually along **sensory and motor pathways** linking the body to
the brain. Studied anatomically, physiologically, behaviorally, and computationally.

## Basic divisions of the nervous system
- **CNS** = brain + spinal cord (+ **retina**). **PNS** = nerves, ganglia, sensory receptors, effectors.
- **Sensory (afferent)** components carry signals *in*; **motor (efferent)** components carry commands *out*.
- Motor splits into **somatic** (→ skeletal/striated muscle) and **visceral/autonomic** (sympathetic, parasympathetic,
  enteric → smooth/cardiac muscle + glands). *(Autonomic system is the Wk15 capstone, Ch 21.)*

## Neurodevelopment (why the brain is shaped the way it is)
Neural tissue derives from **ectoderm → neuroectoderm**. Timeline: **neural plate (~18d) → neural groove/folds → neural
tube + neural crest (~20–22d)**. The **neural tube** becomes the CNS; its **lumen becomes the ventricles**; **neural
crest** becomes much of the PNS (sensory/autonomic ganglia).
- **Three primary vesicles → adult brain** (know this table cold):
  - **Prosencephalon (forebrain)** → **Telencephalon** (cerebral cortex + basal ganglia/amygdala; *lateral ventricles*)
    + **Diencephalon** (thalamus, hypothalamus, retina; *third ventricle*)
  - **Mesencephalon (midbrain)** → colliculi, red nucleus, substantia nigra (*cerebral aqueduct*)
  - **Rhombencephalon (hindbrain)** → **Metencephalon** (cerebellum + pons) + **Myelencephalon** (medulla) (*fourth ventricle*)
  - **Spinal cord** (*central canal*)

## Cells of the nervous system
- **Neurons** — primary signal processors; "excitable" (bioelectric), diverse morphology, specialized for communication.
  Zones: **input** (dendrites/soma) → **conducting** (axon) → **output** (synaptic terminals).
- **Neuroglia** — support/modulate neurons: **astrocytes** (chemical support, blood-brain barrier, scars), **oligodendrocytes**
  (**myelin** → fast conduction), **microglia** (resident immune cells, inflammation, wound healing).
- **Vascular endothelium** — blood supply + blood-brain barrier.

## Gray vs. white matter
- **Gray matter** = neuron cell bodies, dendrites, synapses, glia (the *processing*). **White matter** = myelinated axons
  (the *wiring*; white from myelin). Cortex = gray outside, white tracts inside; nuclei = gray islands within white.

## Surface anatomy of the cerebral hemispheres (localization)
- **Four lobes** + landmarks: **central sulcus** (frontal|parietal border), **lateral (Sylvian) fissure** (frontal/parietal
  above | temporal below), **parieto-occipital sulcus**, **preoccipital notch**.
- Rough functional themes: **frontal** = action/decisions/planning/regulation; **parietal** = bodily sensation, spatial
  awareness, body schema; **occipital** = vision; **temporal** = hearing, speech, recognition, memory; **cingulate/medial**
  = introspection/mind-wandering; **insula** (deep in Sylvian fissure) = taste, visceral/"gut" feelings.
- Key gyri to ID: **precentral gyrus** (primary motor) & **postcentral gyrus** (primary somatosensory) flanking the central sulcus.
- **Subcortical** (mid-sagittal): thalamus, hypothalamus, corpus callosum, fornix, anterior/posterior commissures, pineal,
  colliculi, mammillary bodies, optic chiasm.

## Ventricles & meninges & vasculature (lab-relevant)
- **Ventricles** (ex-neural-tube lumen): lateral → (foramen of Monro) → third → (cerebral aqueduct) → fourth → central canal.
  **Choroid plexus** makes CSF.
- **Meninges** (outside→in): **dura → arachnoid → pia**; CSF sits in the **subarachnoid space**.
- **Arterial supply:** **anterior circulation = internal carotid**; **posterior circulation = vertebral → basilar**; they join
  in the **Circle of Willis** (ACA/MCA/PCA territories). **Venous drainage:** dural sinuses → internal jugular.

## Anatomical axes & planes (used every lab)
- Directions: **rostral/caudal, dorsal/ventral, anterior/posterior, superior/inferior** — note the axis *bends* ~90° at the
  midbrain (forebrain longitudinal axis vs. brainstem/cord axis).
- Section **planes:** **coronal (frontal), sagittal, horizontal (axial)** — the same planes you slice specimens and read scans in.
