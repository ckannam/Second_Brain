## The TBL weekly rhythm (how the course works)
**Mon lecture → study lecture + readings → Wed Readiness Assurance → Thu/Fri lab.** Readiness Assurance = an
**individual quiz (iRA, 10–15 Qs) in Canvas at the start of Wednesday**, immediately redone as a **team (tRA)** with
scratch-off immediate feedback (don't random-guess — each extra scratch loses points). Then Q&A + lab preview.
**Labs are team-based** (real human brain specimens + physiology experiments on teammates: nerve conduction, EMG, EOG,
sensory testing) and produce a **team Lab Report**.
