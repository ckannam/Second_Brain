## How the lanes interlock (the one dependency that matters)

The two lanes meet at the **SBIR**: a **submitted** federal proposal triggers the **One NC
Incentive** (done via the pending Navy NV068), and an **executed Phase I award** triggers the
**One NC Match** (50% up to $75K). So the single highest-leverage event on this calendar is
**winning a Phase I** — Navy (pending) or an xTech-finalist Army Phase I (Mar–Apr 2027) — because
it unlocks the biggest state money downstream. Full logic in [[atp-nc-nondilutive-funding]] §2–3.
