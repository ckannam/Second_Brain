# DoD SBIR / Federal Funding Landscape — Human Performance

A mechanism map for early-stage human-performance startups pursuing non-dilutive DoD
funding. Companion to the live monitoring in [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]]; this
page is the **static conceptual frame** (mechanisms, entry criteria, timing patterns)
while that page holds the running watch-and-act notes.

---

## Why this landscape is unusual

Human performance sits at the intersection of **biomedical**, **training**, and **human
factors** — three distinct SBIR topic families that rarely appear in the same release.
Most civilian health SBIR programs (NIH, NSF) fund *disease* outcomes; DoD funds
*operational readiness* outcomes. The vocabulary shift (commander → readiness →
warfighter → sustainment) is the unlock.

Key structural feature: DoD SBIR is a **topic-driven competition**. A startup cannot
apply without a matching topic. All strategy flows from topic surveillance + early
contact with authors.

## Mechanism map

### 1 — DSIP (Defense SBIR/STTR Innovation Portal) — topic-driven

**What it is:** The DoD-wide portal aggregating Phase I proposals across all service
components. Each release cycle pre-releases topics 2–4 weeks before open, then accepts
proposals for ~6–8 weeks.

**Cadence:** 2–3 releases/year. FY27 pattern so far:
- R1: pre-released ~Oct 7, opened Oct 28, closes **Nov 25, 2026** (12 PM ET)
- R2: pre-released Nov 3–4, opens **Dec 2, 2026**, closes **Jan 6, 2027** (12 PM ET)

**Human-performance home:** 711th Human Performance Wing (711 HPW/RH), Air Force.
Also DARPA Biological Technologies Office, Army DEVCOM Soldier Center (NSRDEC).

**Topic-author contact window:** Opens at pre-release, closes at proposal open. This is
the highest-leverage moment — a 15-min call with the topic author before close dramatically
raises fit assessment and sometimes uncovers informal follow-on paths.

**Phase I:** ~$275–$300K / 12 months. Phase II: ~$1.5–2M / 24 months.

**Verdict for ATP:** Monitor every release. No matching topic in FY27 R1. FY27 R2
pre-released Nov 3–4 — scan now. Historical base rate: 711 HPW topics appear in roughly
1 of every 3 releases; patience + relationship-building fills the gap years.

---

### 2 — AFWERX Focused Open Topics (FOTs) — open-topic competition

**What it is:** Air Force / AFWERX open-topic SBIR/STTR (not tied to a predefined topic).
Any dual-use technology can apply if the pitch fits the Air Force mission lens.

**Cadence:** Rolling releases, typically 4–6 FOTs/year. Each FOT has its own tech focus
area; human performance appears only in some (look for "human factors", "human systems
integration", "readiness", "performance optimization").

**Key difference from DSIP:** Applicant picks their technology focus — no topic match
required. The tradeoff: the bar for demonstrating Air Force relevance is entirely on the
applicant.

**Phase I / II:** Same ceiling as DSIP. Accepts simultaneous Phase I+II ("Direct to
Phase II") in some cycles.

**Verdict for ATP:** A strong fallback when no DSIP topic matches. Frame around "Aircrew
Readiness Fingerprint" (the ATP angle most legible to 711 HPW/AFWERX reviewers).
Watch for the AFWERX FOT pre-release calendar at [afwerx.com](https://afwerx.com).

---

### 3 — SOCOM OSBP Standing BAA — rolling submission

**What it is:** USSOCOM Office of Small Business Programs maintains a standing Broad
Agency Announcement for biomedical and human-performance technologies. Rolling
submissions accepted throughout the period of performance.

**Current BAA:** HT9425-23-S-SOC1 (FY23–FY28). Closes **July 31, 2028**.

**Human performance fit:** SOF operators are the most demanding human-performance
customer in DoD. POTFF (Preservation of the Force and Family) is the umbrella program.
Adjacent primes: sports-medicine, S&C, nutrition contractors already embedded with SOF
units.

**Entry path:** Submit a white paper to discovery@socom.mil. Relationships via SOFWERX
(Tampa) are a strong pre-submission warm channel — the SOFWERX OSBP networking events
(e.g., Nov 2026 event) exist specifically to bridge primes, investors, and small
businesses to SOCOM program offices.

**Verdict for ATP:** Highest warfighter-relevance but highest barrier (SOF customer is
exacting). Best entered after the Navy SBIR Phase I is won (adds credibility). Submit
the SOFWERX capability profile now as the pipeline-building move; white paper when
Phase I is in hand.

---

### 4 — xTech competitions — prize + SBIR bridge

**What it is:** Army series of prize competitions ($1M cash) that run a 3-stage funnel
(white paper → pitch → finals) on open topics, then winners can fast-track into Army
SBIR. Not a classic topic-driven program — any dual-use tech with an Army mission angle.

**xTech|Search 10 (current):**
- Submissions closed Oct 19, 2026 ✅
- Virtual pitch presentations: **January 2027**
- Winner announced: **March 4, 2027**
- Prize: $1M cash + up to $6M SBIR Phase II bridging

**Key eligibility gate for ATP:** >50% US-citizen/PR ownership. Disclose the Navy SBIR
("not substantially the same" standard). Valid Eval portal (not DSIP).

**Verdict for ATP:** xTech is the highest-upside near-term prize path. xTech|Search 11
expected mid-2027 — keep the white paper template warm.

---

### 5 — MTEC (Medical Technology Enterprise Consortium) — applied research

**What it is:** Army-funded consortium vehicle for medical technology R&D. Periodic
multi-topic RPPs (Requests for Proposals/Projects) with focus areas defined by Army
medical command.

**Human performance fit:** Musculoskeletal (MSK) health, human factors, operational
medicine research. Focus Area 10B (MSK) appeared in the FY26 multi-topic RPP (deadline
Oct 30, 2026).

**Entry bar:** MTEC membership required. TRL ≥ 3 + preliminary data. Clinical partner
strongly preferred (Johns Hopkins / JHTV is a natural match).

**Verdict for ATP:** Medium-term play. Build the clinical partnership first; MTEC
membership and an RPP submission become tractable once ATP has IRB-collected preliminary
data.

## Cross-mechanism strategy for ATP (priority stack)

1. **Win the Navy SBIR Phase I** — the credential that unlocks every downstream path.
2. **Scan DSIP FY27 R2 immediately** (opens Dec 2) — look for any adjacent
   human-performance, training-load, or readiness topic; contact author during the window.
3. **File SOFWERX capability profile** — relationship-building while waiting for a topic
   match; direct line to SOCOM POTFF primes.
4. **Watch xTech|Search 10 results (Jan–Mar 2027)** — template the white paper for
   xTech 11.
5. **AFWERX FOT surveillance** — fallback if no DSIP topic match in FY27 R1/R2.
6. **MTEC medium-term** — pursue once clinical preliminary data is in hand.
