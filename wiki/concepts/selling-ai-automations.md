# Selling AI Automations

The business half of the vault: how [[nate-herk]] (and guests) turn [[agentic-workflows]] into income.

## Core playbook
- **Sell the outcome first, build after commitment.** Lead with a [[zero-risk-offer]] (free pilot / case-study-for-reference) to beat the trust barrier — the free client becomes the first paying client and the social proof compounds. Srcs: [[college-student-500k-cold-email]] (Suvam, $500k in 6 mo), [[sign-first-ai-client-7-days]] (Christian, 7-day plan).
- **Sell boring, high-demand workflows** — lead reactivation, CRM "hidden money," sticky/retention automations — not flashy ones. Src: [[500-ai-workflows-businesses-want]].
- **Find niche, pre-filtered lead databases** + simple 4-step personalization at scale (cold email).
- **Price on value:** agentic workflows as $10k+ deliverables ([[how-to-build-10k-agentic-workflows]]); animated websites for thousands ([[nano-banana-2-claude-code-10k-websites]]).
- Full arc — mindset, finding clients, first client in 7 days, pricing, delivery — in [[build-sell-claude-code-course]].

## Self-optimizing deliverables ([[autoresearch]])
The autoresearch pattern turns a one-time build into a **product that improves itself** — a strong selling angle. [[nick-saraev]] wraps it as a cold-email reply-rate optimizer ([[claude-code-karpathy-autoresearch-nick-saraev]]); [[greg-isenberg]] lists 10 businesses on top of it ([[autoresearch-broke-internet-greg-isenberg]]) — niche "agent-in-a-box" tools, "we run 100× more tests" A/B agencies, research-as-a-service, an "optimize" button inside your SaaS. The pitch writes itself: *"this runs experiments for you 24/7 and just shows you the winner."* Caveat: it only works where "better" is an objective, cheaply-measured metric (see [[autoresearch]]).
