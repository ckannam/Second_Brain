# AI for science

Anthropic's thesis (from [[the-briefing-ai-for-science]]) that AI can **compress 50–100 years
of biology/medicine progress into 5–10** — voiced two years earlier by the CEO as ambition,
now claimed to be starting. Aim: "**10 years of progress every year**."

The mechanism isn't AI replacing science — it's **collapsing the toil**. An experiment might
take 3 days to run but 3+ weeks to analyze; "those weeks aren't science, they're the toil you
endure to get to the science." AI provides starting points a human "would never have thought
of," while humans keep the experimental rigor, creativity, and discipline. Enabling need:
"**bilingual**" people fluent in both a scientific domain and AI.

Productized as **[[claude-science|Claude Science]]** — an AI workbench with full
reproducibility (code + environment + conversation per artifact) and 60+ scientific
databases. Kin to [[elicit]]'s research assistant. Related: [[rag]], [[the-capability-curve]].
