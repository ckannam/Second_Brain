# Temptation bundling

Strategy #1 for [[discipline-without-willpower]]. Pair a task you **have** to do (the hard
thing) with a task you **enjoy** (the reward), bringing the reward into the immediate
present to counter [[temporal-discounting]].

Examples ([[erin-meryl]]): only allow a favorite playlist while running; only allow a
nice iced coffee while studying.

Companion tactic: **break hard tasks into the smallest possible units** and check them
off. Each completion delivers a small dopamine hit, and because motivation is tied to
dopamine release, follow-through on subsequent tasks increases.

Research: Milkman et al. 2014, "Holding the Hunger Games Hostage at the Gym."

Source: [[discipline-without-willpower-erin-meryl]].
