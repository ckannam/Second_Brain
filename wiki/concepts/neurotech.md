# Neurotech

The **neurotech** (neurotechnology) industry sits at the intersection of **neuroscience** and **engineering/AI**: tools that read, write to, or replicate the brain — spanning brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), AI systems inspired by neural architecture, neurostimulation devices, and computational neuroscience platforms. As of 2026 it is one of the fastest-funded deep-tech verticals.

## Market snapshot (2026)

- **Global neurotechnology market:** projected to cross **$50B by 2034** (DelveInsight); current run-rate $10–12B.
- **BCI funding:** $523M raised in BCI startups in 2026 YTD; Q1 2025–Q1 2026 total = **$2.36B** across 30 public rounds.
- **Geography:** North America captures **76%** of disclosed BCI capital ($1.57B over 24 months).
- **234 active BCI companies** as of April 2026; 166 have received funding.
- Investor focus has shifted from "BCI concept" pitches to **deployment credibility** and **platform optionality**.

Sources: DelveInsight market report; NewMarketPitch BCI funding dataset (2026).

## The brain-inspired AI wave (2026)

A distinct sub-sector has emerged: companies using **wet-lab neuroscience to directly inform AI architecture**, not just metaphorically. The core bet: human brains operate on ~20–50 watts and learn continuously from sparse data; current GPU clusters are 10,000× less efficient. If you can decode the brain's "core algorithm" from connectomics + cortical-column experiments, you can rebuild AI on that architecture.

**[[Flourish]]** is the highest-profile example: $500M raised (Bezos anchor, Lux, GV, [[catalio-capital|Catalio]]); targeting a **hippocampus-inspired memory** that learns without large training data, plus a continuously-learning model for pocket devices. Co-founded by JHU's **[[Joshua Vogelstein]]** (connectome scientist). See [[flourish-wired-core-algorithm]] for the source.

The same "foundation-model recipe applied to a domain" pattern recurs: [[kronos-financial-foundation-model|Kronos]] → financial markets; Chai → antibodies. Brain is the highest-value target.
