# Multimodal RAG

[[rag|Retrieval-augmented generation]] over images, video, and text together, made turnkey by [[gemini-embeddings-2]] + [[pinecone]] + [[claude-code]] ([[google-rag-gemini-embeddings-2]]) — no hand-built chunking/ingestion. Contrast the text-first [[llm-wiki-vs-rag|LLM Wiki]] approach.
