# Claude Code Channels

Messaging channels that let you reach a [[claude-code]] session from outside the terminal — notably the **[[claude-code-imessage|iMessage channel]]** (this vault is operated through it). Launch in "channel mode" and allow specific phone numbers.

One of three remote-access options, distinguished in [[claude-code-imessage]]: **[[dispatch|Dispatch]] vs Channels vs [[claude-code-remote-control|Remote Control]]**.
