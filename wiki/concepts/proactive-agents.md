# Proactive Agents

Agents that act **without being asked** — checking in, surfacing help, and building things in the background — rather than only responding to prompts. The defining trait of the "Klaus" [[ai-executive-assistant|assistant]] on [[clawdbot]] ([[i-turned-clawdbot-personal-assistant]]), enabled by [[claude-code-scheduled-tasks|scheduled tasks]]/loops and [[claude-code-memory|memory]].

**Anthropic's first-party version — [[claude-tag|Claude Tag / @Claude]]** ([[future-of-work-claude-tag]]): "in the past you opened Claude and asked; now Claude jumps in." Proactive *and* **multiplayer** (add it to a Slack channel; the whole team participates), runs work over days/weeks, follows up, and remembers next time.
