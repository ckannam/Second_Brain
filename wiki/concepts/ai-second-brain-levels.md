# AI Second Brain — The 5 Levels

From [[every-level-claude-second-brain]]: a [[claude-code]] second brain has **five levels**, and the goal is the *lowest level that solves your pain*, not level 5.

1. **Level 1** — a single [[claude-md-router|`CLAUDE.md` router]] (context + where to look).
2. **Level 2** — organized context files / structured project.
3. **Level 3** — [[claude-code-skills|skills]] & reusable workflows.
4. **Level 4** — [[claude-code-subagents|sub-agents]] / delegation.
5. **Level 5** — always-on autonomous system ([[claude-code-scheduled-tasks|scheduled tasks]], loops, self-improvement).

This vault itself is a second brain built on the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] (a Level 2–3 knowledge system) reachable over the [[claude-code-imessage|iMessage channel]] (a Level 5 access pattern). Related: [[second-brain-system]], [[ai-executive-assistant]].
