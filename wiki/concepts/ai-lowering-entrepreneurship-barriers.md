# AI lowering the barriers to entrepreneurship

The **research thesis** behind Cole's [[fulbright-us-student-program|Fulbright]] Open Study/Research proposal: **AI is enabling entrepreneurship in contexts and economies where founding a company wasn't previously possible** — by lowering the traditional barriers to starting a business:

- **Capital** — cheaper to build and validate a product before raising money.
- **Expertise** — AI substitutes for skills a solo/first-time founder would otherwise have to hire (engineering, design, legal drafting, market research).
- **Infrastructure** — cloud + AI tooling removes fixed costs and local-ecosystem gaps that previously gated who could start.

The interesting frontier is **where** this matters most: emerging / underserved economies, where the pre-AI barriers were highest, so the marginal unlock from AI is largest. That framing is what makes a **single-country, on-the-ground research project** coherent (see [[fulbright-country-selection]] for how India / Brazil / Indonesia each instantiate it).
