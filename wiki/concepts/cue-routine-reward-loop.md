# Cue-routine-reward loop

Strategy #2 for [[discipline-without-willpower]]: **automate** the start of a hard task so
the brain faces less friction deciding to begin. Uses conditioning (classical
conditioning / Pavlov): a consistent **cue** preconditions the brain for the **routine**,
followed by a **reward**.

Example ([[erin-meryl]]): making/getting an iced coffee becomes the cue that a study
session is next, so sitting down to study meets far less internal resistance. Overlaps
with [[temptation-bundling]] (the reward) and [[environment-design]] (arranging cues).

Reduces reliance on willpower by making the behavior semi-automatic rather than a fresh
decision each time.

Source: [[discipline-without-willpower-erin-meryl]].
