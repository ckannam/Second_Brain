# Translational / Non-Dilutive Funding (JHTV)

Funding that sets early-stage discoveries on a path to commercialization by **de-risking** the
tech and raising its value to commercial partners — **without diluting equity** (may carry
participation/payback terms). The landscape Cole's [[jhtv-grant-finder]] tracks.

## Key programs (at/around [[jhtv|JHTV]])
- **Cohen, Thalheimer & Zizic Funds** — three philanthropic non-dilutive funds via a **single
  application**, offered twice/year, right-sized to the next milestone.
- **President's Innovation Award** — annual, for one early/mid-career faculty innovator.
- **Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII)** — via the state's **TEDCO**.
- **Maryland Stem Cell Research Fund (MSCRF)** — TEDCO, stem-cell commercialization.
- **Bluefield Innovations** — Deerfield Management × JHU, early-stage therapeutics.
- **Blaze / Pava Center** — student & alumni venture funding + mentorship.

Complements equity funding (VCs) — see [[capital-strategy]] and [[vc-matching-second-brain]].
