# Temporal discounting

The brain's dopamine system is a present-biased machine: it **steeply discounts future
rewards**. Example ([[erin-meryl]]): most people value £100 today over £200 next week,
even while claiming they'd take the £200.

This is the root cause — over 90% — of why [[discipline-without-willpower|discipline
feels hard]]: for the gym, study, etc., the **cost is in the present** (fatigue, effort)
while the **reward is in the future** (physique, exam results). Because the discount is
hardwired, willpower-based "think about your long-term goals" advice fails.

The practical countermeasures all work by **pulling the reward into the present** or
lowering present cost: [[temptation-bundling]], [[cue-routine-reward-loop]],
[[environment-design]].
