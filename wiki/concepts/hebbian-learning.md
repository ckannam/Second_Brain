# Hebbian learning (attention shapes the brain)

**"Neurons that fire together, wire together."** When you focus attention on something you
activate specific neural circuits, and circuits active at the same time form stronger
connections. From [[neuroplasticity-rewiring-brain-ixcarus]].

- **Attention is the selector for [[neuroplasticity]].** Where you direct attention determines
  what the brain optimizes for. Attend to negativity → stronger negativity/scarcity pathways;
  attend to distraction → weakened sustained-focus circuits (why doom-scrolling is
  neurologically damaging — it trains anxiety and distractibility).
- The reverse is the lever: deliberately attend to what you want to reinforce (gratitude,
  single-task focus) and the brain builds those circuits and starts scanning for them.
- **Mindfulness/meditation = attention training**, not woo — you practice directing and
  sustaining attention, strengthening the circuits that control attention, which transfer
  everywhere. "The ability to control your attention is the ability to control what your brain
  reinforces."

Attention also programs *perception* via the [[reticular-activating-system]], and is how
[[knowledge-types|conceptual learning]] and [[memory-consolidation|encoding]] happen at all.
Pairs with [[environment-design]] (control the cues that capture attention).
