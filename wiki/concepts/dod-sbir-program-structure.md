# DoD SBIR / STTR Program Structure

The **Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR)** and **Small Business Technology Transfer
(STTR)** programs are the federal government's primary mechanism for funding R&D at small
businesses toward defense applications. The DoD manages the largest SBIR program across the
federal government; every military branch participates.

> **Synthesis note:** This page consolidates the DoD SBIR structure from the vault's ATP federal
> funding research ([[atp-federal-funding-landscape]]). Most figures were web-verified in context
> of ATP's Navy SBIR submission (Oct 2026). Treat the program specifics as a snapshot — SBIR was
> reauthorized Apr 13, 2026 (through Sep 30, 2031) with rule changes; check primary sources
> (dodsbirsttr.mil, afwerx.com, SAM.gov) before submitting.

## The phase pathway

SBIR has three phases; companies enter at Phase I and can progress through the pipeline:

| Phase | Purpose | Typical ceiling | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase I** | Feasibility study | ~$75K–$305K (varies by agency/topic) | 6–12 months |
| **Phase II** | Full R&D + prototype | ~$1.7M–$2M | 24 months |
| **Phase III** | Commercialization / scale | No SBIR funding (uses non-SBIR DoD contracts or commercial) | Ongoing |

STTR structure is identical, but requires a formal partnership with a research institution (e.g.
a university). **Phase III is where the government actually *buys* what it funded** — a
contractor holding a Phase II can be sole-sourced into Phase III without competition.

## The monthly release cadence (DoD DSIP)

Since ~2026, DoD SBIR/STTR topics **pre-release on the first Wednesday of each month** via the
Defense SBIR/STTR Innovation Portal (**DSIP**, dodsbirsttr.mil). The release cycle:

1. **Pre-release (first Wednesday):** Topic list published; a "topic author contact" window opens
   so companies can reach the government scientist directly — this is the relationship-building
   window, not a submission window.
2. **Opens (~4 weeks after pre-release):** Proposal submission opens. The window is typically
   ~4 weeks long.
3. **Closes:** Submission deadline. Proposals reviewed; awards announced over the following
   months.

Before the monthly model, releases were batched seasonally (1–3 per year). The monthly cadence
makes SBIR a standing pipeline rather than a scramble.

**Example — FY27 Release 1:** pre-released Oct 7, 2026; opens Oct 28; closes Nov 25, 2026.
FY27 R1 contained 25 topics: 16 DoAF (13 SBIR + 3 STTR), 6 Navy, 2 OSW, 1 Army.

## Branches and program offices

Each military branch has its own SBIR program within the DoD umbrella:

- **Air Force / DoAF (AFWERX):** manages DoAF SBIR/STTR topics. Historically had an
  "Open Topic" (propose-anything); as of 2026 replaced with **Focused Open Topics** (the Air
  Force defines the capability gap, companies propose solutions). The 711th Human Performance
  Wing is the highest-fit Air Force customer for human-performance technology.
- **Navy:** managed via NAVSEA/ONR; topics published on DSIP. ATP's pending proposal
  (DON26BZ05-NV068, "Intelligent Tools for Naval Aircrew Performance and Readiness") is in the
  Navy lane.
- **Army:** managed via ARO/ERDC; also includes the **xTech|Search** prize competition (adjacent
  vehicle, parallel pathway to Army SBIR).
- **SOCOM:** has its own standing BAA for biomedical + human performance R&D (FY23–FY28,
  HT9425-23-S-SOC1) — rolling submissions, not tied to the monthly DSIP cycle. This is the most
  direct-fit mechanism for human-performance companies.

## TACFI / STRATFI (Air Force scale-up vehicles)

**TACFI** (Tactical Funding Increase) and **STRATFI** (Strategic Funding Increase) are
Department of the Air Force (DoAF) vehicles that let a Phase II awardee scale to tens of
millions in matched government + non-dilutive commercial funding. Key constraints:
- **DoAF only** — ATP's Navy SBIR (DON26BZ05-NV068) cannot convert to TACFI/STRATFI. These
  require a DoAF (AFWERX / 711 HPW) SBIR Phase II as the entry point.
- **Requires Phase II + a named DoD champion.** The relationship-first moves (711 HPW CHEERS,
  the Barbara Knight contact, an AFWERX Focused Open Topic) are the prerequisites — TACFI/
  STRATFI are downstream rewards for a company that already has a DoAF champion.

## Key eligibility rules

- **US small business:** ≤500 employees (most programs), for-profit, majority-owned by US
  citizens or permanent residents (>50%), or by a venture capital company / hedge fund (but
  the >50% US-citizen control rule applies to xTech and some programs separately).
- **"Substantially the same" rule:** a pending proposal at another agency counts as
  disclosure-required. Companies with parallel proposals across branches must disclose and show
  scope differentiation.
- **CMMC Level 1/2 self-assessment** required for AFWERX solicitations until Nov 10, 2026.
- **New (2026 reauthorization):** per-firm Phase I caps; foreign-risk/supply-chain screening;
  a Phase II "strategic breakthrough" vehicle ($30M ceiling, requires POM commitment + ≥20%
  new non-SBIR DoD match).
