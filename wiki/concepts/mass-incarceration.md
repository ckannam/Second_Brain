# Mass incarceration (US carceral system)

The systemic-justice subject that sits under the **"useful / help others"** half of Cole's identity
([[cole]]) — grounded in his parents' Baltimore public service and his own **City of Baltimore /
Deputy Mayor** human-services + opioid work. Seeded by the substantive first half of

**values context, not a career lane.**

## The mechanics (why the system reproduces itself)
- **Scale & composition:** the US has the developed world's highest incarceration rate — on the order of
  **1.8M** incarcerated, a **majority non-violent** offenders (Freleng cites ~70–80%). Much of it traces
  to 1990s drug-war sentencing.
- **Mandatory sentencing removed judicial discretion.** California's **1994 "three strikes"** law and the
  **"super-predator"** panic produced **juvenile life-without-parole** under mandatory schemes — judges
  *couldn't* weigh a child's circumstances. The Supreme Court found mandatory JLWOP unconstitutional only
  in **2012 (Miller v. Alabama)** / **2016 (Montgomery)** [general-knowledge anchor for Freleng's "2014/2017"
  framing]. Example: **"Suave,"** sentenced at 17, later released — the subject of Freleng's Pulitzer-winning
  podcast.
- **Wrongful convictions** are ~**10%** of the prison population by her estimate; some rest on **discredited
  forensics** (e.g. shaken-baby cases where no crime occurred).
- **Re-entry is engineered to fail.** Parole you must **pay for**; **lifetime parole** where a misdemeanor
  or a missed payment re-incarcerates you; **child-support debt** (Louisiana) that blocks an ID or job over
  a ~$1,500 balance → the street economy → recidivism. The "high recidivism" then gets used to argue *against*
  release — when the real cause is withheld tools for re-entry.
- **The profit layer:** **CoreCivic** and **GEO Group** run private prisons *and* ICE detention on a
  **per-diem bed-rate** (paid per person held → structural incentive to lock up and hold), with **labor the
  biggest cost slice** and near-free **prison labor** (Angola — literally a former plantation). ICE detention
  **oversees itself**, unlike DOJ facilities.
