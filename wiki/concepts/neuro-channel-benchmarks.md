# Neuro channel — competitor & format benchmarks

Format research for the **[[neuro-channel|Neuro]]** venture: how the channels that already win at
short-form neuroscience/psychology explainers structure their hooks, pacing, titles, and
positioning — so Neuro's doodle-Shorts inherit proven patterns instead of guessing. Companion to
[[neuro-channel]] (the hub), [[neuro-production-pipeline]] (how videos get made), and
[[neuro-free-tool-stack]] (the $0 stack). Built @cloud 2026-07-30 from web research; refill it as the
landscape shifts.

## The benchmarked channels

| Channel | Scale | Format & style | Why it's a model for Neuro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Psych2Go** | **~12.9M** subs, 2B+ views | Bite-sized **2D-animated** psychology / mental-health; calm narration; sprout mascot; **listicle hooks** ("*X signs of…*") | The category king. Proves animated psych scales massively; the **curiosity-gap listicle title** is the workhorse hook to study. |
| **Neuroscientifically Challenged** | **~399k** subs | **"2-Minute Neuroscience"** — one concept per video, ~2 min, simple hand-drawn/whiteboard visuals, rigidly consistent series naming | Closest **structural** model: single-concept scoping, cheap hand-drawn visuals, no on-camera face, a named repeatable series. Almost exactly Neuro's constraints (Prof. Marc Dingman, Penn State). |
| **Neuro Transmissions** | **~186k** subs | Friendly brain/psych explainers; mix of hosts + animation (Alie Astrocyte + Micah) | Shows the "two-hosts + light animation" lane; warmer, personality-led — a contrast to Neuro's character-led doodle approach. |
| **Sprouts** | large edu channel | **Doodle-on-paper cartoons** explaining complex ideas simply (incl. learning science) | Closest **aesthetic** match to Neuro's hand-drawn look; proves the whiteboard-doodle style reads as credible, not cheap. |

## Patterns that transfer to Neuro's Shorts
- **Scope one idea per video.** The 2-Minute-Neuroscience discipline — a Short answers exactly one
  "why does the brain do X" question. It keeps scripts tight and the topic bank ([[neuro-channel]])
  maps 1:1 to episodes.
- **Lead with a curiosity-gap hook, not a definition.** Psych2Go's opener names a *felt* experience
  and withholds the mechanism ("*Ever walk into a room and forget why?*") — the answer is the payoff.
  Neuro's "why you…" topics are already built for this.
- **Consistent series branding.** A fixed name + intro + visual signature (Neuroscientifically
  Challenged's "2-Minute Neuroscience"; Psych2Go's mascot) makes the feed recognizable and cheap to
  produce. Neuro's walking-brain doodle *is* that signature — use it every episode.
- **Relatable-first, science-second.** Every winner opens on a human/behavioral situation and *earns*
  the neuroscience, rather than opening on anatomy. Match the [[neuroscience-of-behavior]] framing.
- **Format math is on-side.** Shorts <60s show higher engagement, YouTube Shorts has 700M+ daily
  users, and the psychology niche is growing ~25% YoY — a favorable moment to enter with a
  differentiated visual style.

## Where Neuro's wedge is
None of the leaders own **hand-drawn doodle scenes at Shorts length with a recurring character**:
Psych2Go is polished flat 2D, Neuroscientifically Challenged is whiteboard-static, Sprouts is
doodle-but-long. Neuro's [[Zenn0009-style|Zenn-style]] AI-doodle scenes + a pinned recurring
character ([[neuro-character-rig]]) is a genuinely distinct aesthetic in the Shorts feed — the
differentiation, not just another animated-psych channel.
