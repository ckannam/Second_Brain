# Reticular activating system (RAS)

A bundle of neurons at the brainstem that acts as the **bridge between conscious and
subconscious** — it decides which of the ~11 million bits/sec of sensory input reach your
~40–50 bits/sec of conscious awareness. From [[conscious-subconscious-mind-ixcarus]].

- **You program it with conscious focus.** Whatever you consciously mark as important, the RAS
  starts surfacing more of. Buy a new car → suddenly see it everywhere (the cars were always
  there; the filter changed). Same mechanism behind "when you fear something you notice it
  everywhere."
- It is **[[cognitive-biases|confirmation bias]] on autopilot** — it shows you evidence for what
  you've told it matters. Focus on problems → it finds problems; focus on solutions/opportunities
  → it surfaces those. Same reality, different filter.
- The practical, non-"manifestation" reading: **directing attention is directing perception.**
  This is the filtering face of [[predictive-processing]] and a specific application of
  [[hebbian-learning|attention shapes the brain]].

Actionable for Cole: set an explicit focus (a question, a goal, a metric) and the RAS recruits
perception toward it — the honest core under "what you focus on grows."
