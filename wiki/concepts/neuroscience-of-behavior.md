# Neuroscience of behavior (hub)

Cole's growing cluster for **grounding behavior and decisions in brain mechanisms** — the
"here's what your brain is actually doing, so do X" layer under the [[discipline-without-willpower|
discipline]] cluster. He wants behaviors *justified in neuroscience*, and this is the entry point.
Seeded by three sources ([[neuroplasticity-rewiring-brain-ixcarus]],
[[how-to-remember-everything-brainhealthdecoded]], [[conscious-subconscious-mind-ixcarus]]);
expected to grow.

## How change actually works (mechanism → tactic)

- **[[neuroplasticity]]** — the brain rewires around whatever you repeat (value-neutral; myelin).
  The hub mechanism. → repetition > intensity; "just show up."
- **[[predictive-processing]]** — the brain predicts you'll act as you did before, and makes it
  true; updates only on behavioral *evidence*. → change identity via accumulated action.
- **[[productive-discomfort]]** — plasticity needs challenge (norepinephrine/acetylcholine);
  comfort is stagnant. → train at the edge; discomfort = the signal it's working.
- **[[hebbian-learning]]** — neurons that fire together wire together; attention is the selector.
  → guard attention; it's what you build.
- **[[memory-consolidation]]** — learning locks in during rest/sleep, not during effort. →
  space practice; protect sleep; process before moving on.

## Learning, faster (from the memory sources)

- **[[learning-by-connection]]** — adults learn by connecting to prior knowledge, not memorizing
  (the human mirror of this vault's [[llm-wiki-pattern|link-first]] philosophy).
- **[[knowledge-types]]** — skill vs concept vs fact each need a different strategy.
- **[[spacing-effect]]** — distributed practice beats massing (robust, domain-general, molecular:
  CREB/ERK) → space sessions; the optimum is computable. · **[[retrieval-practice]]** — testing >
  restudy (but not robust in math) · **[[interleaving]]** — mixed practice + discriminative
  contrast · **[[spaced-repetition]]** — the applied technique/tools (Anki/FSRS, [[spacerep]]).
  This sub-cluster is the science behind Cole's [[neuro-channel|learning-app]] idea.

## The mind that's running the show

- **[[conscious-vs-subconscious]]** — a tiny conscious mind (~7±2) riding a vast subconscious
  (~11M bits/s); learning is the handoff from conscious struggle to subconscious automation.
- **[[reticular-activating-system]]** — the attention filter you can program with focus.
- **[[cognitive-biases]]** — the subconscious's fast shortcuts and their systematic errors.
