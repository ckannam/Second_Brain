# Cold Email Outreach

Beginner-friendly client acquisition channel in the vault. [[suvam]]'s framework ([[college-student-500k-cold-email]]): use AI to find **niche, pre-filtered lead databases** (not giant lists) + a simple 4-step personalization automation, and lead with a [[zero-risk-offer]]. Contrast [[christian]]'s warm-outreach 7-day approach ([[sign-first-ai-client-7-days]]). Part of [[selling-ai-automations]].

**Auto-optimizing the copy.** [[nick-saraev]] applies [[autoresearch]] to cold email ([[claude-code-karpathy-autoresearch-nick-saraev]]): metric = reply rate (via the Instantly API), changeable input = the copy, run on a GitHub Actions cron as a **baseline-vs-challenger** loop whose learnings compound in a `resource.md`. Reply rate is a clean fit because it's objective and auto-tracked — exactly the condition autoresearch needs.
