# Environment design

Strategy #4 for [[discipline-without-willpower]]: shape your surroundings so the
disciplined action is the low-friction default and distractions are removed.

Two levers ([[erin-meryl]]):
- **Reduce clutter.** A cluttered environment overloads working memory (which holds few
  items at once), making focus hard. Keep the workspace clear.
- **Reduce friction to the goal.** Do interim steps in advance: lay out running shoes and
  clothes the night before, pull up the essay materials before bed. This is also why
  writing goals the night before helps.

Sits alongside [[cue-routine-reward-loop]] (cues live in the environment) and is a direct
counter to [[temporal-discounting]] by lowering the present cost of starting. Broadly this
is a "nudge" approach (Thaler & Sunstein 2008).

Source: [[discipline-without-willpower-erin-meryl]].
