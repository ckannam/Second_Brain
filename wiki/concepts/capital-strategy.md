# Capital Strategy (JHTV) — Cole's Role

The [[jhtv|JHTV]] **Capital Strategy team** maps the full **funder landscape** — VCs,
corporate, philanthropic, and non-dilutive grants — onto early-stage Hopkins **spinouts**, and
translates science into **investment-ready narratives**. Cole is a **Capital Strategy Intern**
(since Jun 2026), reporting to [[Stewart Neifart]] (team head).

## What Cole actually does
- **Built the [[vc-matching-second-brain|tech→VC auto-matcher]]** — his longest-term project: an
  AI tool matching JHTV technologies to the right investors (JSON-in-git DB, client-side scoring,
  a Claude+web-search backend, JHU-alum warm intros). *His flagship.*
- **Built the [[jhtv-grant-finder]]** — surfaces & tracks non-dilutive [[translational-funding|grants]]
  and deadlines for spinouts (shares the matcher's grant engine).
- **Reviews Translational Funding applications** — evaluates TF applications and maintains the
  review tracker (e.g. the Spring 2026 master tracker across MD/Tech categories). Real
  due-diligence/evaluation work. *(Content confidential — not in the vault.)*
- **Writes application memos / one-pagers** — built a JHTV application **one-pager + template**;
  produces investment/application memos.
- **Maintains a JHU VC network database** + does **market research**, **Excel models**, and
  **market positioning** across the pipeline.

This role *is* Cole's thesis made concrete: **science ↔ capital ↔ AI-building**. He's not just
analyzing deals — he's **automating the funder-matching function with AI tools he built himself**.
That combination (health/bio literacy + venture instinct + shipping real AI software) is his
single strongest credential for the operator→VC recruit ([[Job Search]], [[five-year-plan]]).
