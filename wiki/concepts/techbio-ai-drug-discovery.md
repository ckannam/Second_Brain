# Techbio / AI Drug Discovery (Cole's category POV)

Cole's chosen **industry of specialty** for VC interviews and applications (see [[vc-interview-prep]],
[[vc-investor-applications]], [[dorm room fund]]). This is the written point of view Bessemer's prep
framework asks for. It also connects directly to what Cole learned at [[jhtv]] about the
[[valley-of-death]] and [[translational-funding]], AI is attacking the front half of that gap.

## The problem (why this matters)
- A new drug takes roughly **10 to 15 years** and about **$2.6 billion** all-in to reach market (counting failures).
- About **90% of drugs entering human trials fail.**
- The slowest, most expensive early stretch is **discovery + preclinical** (target ID, hit-to-lead, lead optimization), roughly **4 to 6 years** before a molecule reaches a human.

## Market size
- Roughly **$4 billion in 2026**, growing at a **~25 to 30% CAGR**, projected to **$40B to $160B by 2035** depending on the analyst.
- Estimates vary widely (2026 figures ranged ~$2.9B to $24B across firms). Defensible line: **low-single-digit billions today, compounding ~25 to 30 percent.**

## Timeline compression (the headline)
[[insilico-medicine|Insilico Medicine]] is the cleanest proof point.
- Target discovery to a **preclinical candidate in ~18 months for ~$2.6M** (vs traditional 2.5 to 4.5 years).
- Target discovery to **Phase 1 in under 30 months.**
- First fully AI-discovered and AI-designed drug (**Rentosertib**, idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis) has a **positive Phase 2 readout**, the first real human validation.

**Sharp nuance:** AI compresses **discovery and preclinical, not the clinical trials** (still the long pole). The bigger long-term prize is raising the 90% clinical failure rate by putting better-designed molecules into trials in the first place.

## Companies to know
- **[[isomorphic-labs|Isomorphic Labs]]** — scale leader. DeepMind spinout on AlphaFold, ~**$20B** valuation, **$600M Series A then a $2.1B round**, **$1.75B** Lilly deal. AI-first, licenses the platform, no wet lab of its own.
- **[[insilico-medicine|Insilico Medicine]]** — proof leader. Generative AI, **$293M HKEX IPO (Dec 2025)**, Lilly collaboration **up to $2.75B** (largest AI-era pharma deal). Only one with a Phase 2 human readout.
- **Recursion** — biology-data leader. Public, merged with Exscientia (2024), massive automated "phenomics."
- **Xaira Therapeutics** — wildcard. ~**$1B seed** (largest biotech seed ever), generative biologics.
- **insitro** (Daphne Koller) and **Genesis Therapeutics / Chai Discovery** — biology-first and next-wave AI-biology.

**Framing:** Isomorphic leads on scale, Insilico and Recursion lead on proof, the newer names lead on the next AI-biology wave.
