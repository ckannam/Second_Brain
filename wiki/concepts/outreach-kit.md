# Outreach Kit

The **executable template library** for Cole's job-search outreach — the actual messages at
send time. The underlying rules come from [[cold-email-job-search]] (the playbook); the system
that uses these templates is [[outreach-pipeline]] (the weekly engine).

## Pre-send gate (checklist every message must pass)

Per the Next Play cold-email standard in [[cold-email-job-search]]:

- [ ] ≤ ~200 words total
- [ ] No buzzwords — read it aloud, does it sound human?
- [ ] One specific ask, stated clearly (15-min call, not "let's connect")
- [ ] No vagueness — replace abstractions with specifics ("startups like X")
- [ ] No fake personalization — honest hooks only (finding via funding = fine)
- [ ] Not a job application — opening a conversation, not asking for a role
- [ ] Reply-to is cdk35@duke.edu (for traceability; move to personal after graduation)

## Template A — Fresh-funding / value-first (Tier 1 targets)

**When:** reaching out to a 🟢 startup that recently raised (sourced via [[startup-radar]],
funding feeds, or the [[july-hiring-thread-benlang|July hiring thread]]). Signals you did
your homework without being creepy.

```
Subject: Congrats on the [round] — quick question

Hi [first name],

Congrats on the [Series X / seed round] — [one sentence on what excited you about the company or the round: the thesis, the market, a specific product angle].

I'm a senior at Duke (Neuroscience + Chemistry, May 2027) with hands-on experience [1–2 specific credentials directly relevant to their mission — e.g., "building AI tools for capital strategy at Johns Hopkins Technology Ventures" or "running the data ops for a student venture at 20+ universities"]. I'd love to see if there's a way I could contribute as you scale.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call in the next few weeks?

Cole Kannam
cdk35@duke.edu | [LinkedIn URL]
```

**Adjust per company:** swap the credential sentence for the most relevant proof point (JHTV VC-matcher → brain/biotech; UShip ops scale → logistics/ops roles; second-brain AI work → AI-infra/tool companies).

## Template B — Warm-intro (Lane 0)

**When:** a connector ([[outreach-pipeline]] Lane 0 contacts) offers to make an introduction.
The connector sends their version; Cole sends this as a follow-up *after* the intro lands.

```
Subject: Following up on [Connector]'s intro

Hi [first name],

[Connector] was kind enough to connect us — really appreciate it.

[1 sentence on why this company/role is specifically interesting to you — no generic "excited about the mission."]

I've attached [a relevant work sample or one-liner on the relevant proof point]. Happy to make this as easy as possible — is a 15-minute call an option?

Cole Kannam
cdk35@duke.edu
```

**Keep it short.** The connector's intro did the credentialing work; don't re-litigate it.

---

## Template C — Follow-up (both tiers)

**When:** no reply after ~5–7 business days. Max **one** follow-up per outreach (two total
touches). After that, move on — per the [[cold-email-job-search]] rule: max 1–2 follow-ups.

```
Subject: Re: [original subject line]

Hi [first name],

Just bumping this up — still interested if the timing ever works. No worries if not.

Cole
```

**That's it.** Do not apologize, re-pitch, or add new material. One line is enough.

---

## Template D — Post-conversation thank-you

**When:** after a call or meeting. Same day or within 24 hours.

```
Subject: Thanks for the time, [first name]

Hi [first name],

Really appreciated the conversation — [one specific thing you'll take away or act on].

[Optional: one follow-up point or resource relevant to what you discussed.]

Looking forward to staying in touch.

Cole
```
