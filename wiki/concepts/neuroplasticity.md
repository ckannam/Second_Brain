# Neuroplasticity

The brain's ability to form new neural connections and reorganize existing ones based on
what you do, think, and pay attention to. The central mechanism behind all lasting behavior
change — the reason [[discipline-without-willpower|discipline is structure, not willpower]].
From [[neuroplasticity-rewiring-brain-ixcarus]].

## Core properties

- **Value-neutral.** The brain reinforces whatever you repeat — scrolling TikTok 3h/night or
  reading 3h/night use the *same* mechanism. It doesn't judge; it adapts. So the leverage is
  in *what* you repeat, not in trying harder.
- **Myelin makes habits automatic.** Repeated use wraps a pathway in myelin (a fatty sheath),
  speeding signal transmission until the behavior runs effortlessly. Bad habits feel
  effortless because they run on "superhighways of myelin"; a new habit is an unmyelinated
  path that needs conscious effort every time.
- **The brain is an efficiency machine.** Conscious effort is metabolically expensive
  (glucose + oxygen), so the brain defaults to established, myelinated pathways. The
  resistance you feel starting something new is **energy conservation, not weakness or lack
  of discipline** — the same point [[discipline-without-willpower]] makes about the
  prefrontal cortex.

## Why change is hard (and how to work with it)

Old pathways are more myelinated than new ones, and the brain runs a
[[predictive-processing|predictive model]] that expects you to behave as you have before.
Change means out-competing an entrenched pathway *and* updating the brain's prediction of who
you are ([[identity-led-goals|identity is neurological]]).

Levers that actually rewire, all downstream of this page:
- **Repetition > intensity** — myelin builds layer by layer; consistency (even half-assed
  reps) beats rare bursts. "Just show up." Reinforces [[cue-routine-reward-loop]].
- **[[productive-discomfort]]** — plasticity needs challenge-triggered neuromodulators; comfort
  is neurologically stagnant.
- **Attention** — [[hebbian-learning|neurons that fire together wire together]]; what you
  attend to is what you build.
- **[[environment-design]]** — cues and mirror neurons keep old pathways firing; reshape them.
- **[[memory-consolidation|Sleep]]** — plasticity locks in during sleep, not during the effort.

**Timelines are individual** (18–254 days in the research), not the mythical "21 days."
Reconcile any fixed-number habit claim against this.
