# Tech-authoritarianism (capitalism over democracy)

The worldview that **liberal democracy is an obstacle to progress** and should be constrained or replaced
by an unaccountable, tech-enabled elite — so that capitalism/innovation can run unrestricted. Surfaced in
[[antichrist-story-peter-thiel]]; the vault's **dark mirror** to [[post-scarcity-and-meaning|the optimistic
AI-abundance narrative]].

## The core claim
[[peter-thiel]]: **"freedom and democracy are [no longer] compatible"** (*The Education of a Libertarian*,
2009). His diagnosis — welfare expansion + universal suffrage turned "capitalist democracy" into an
oxymoron because majorities vote for redistribution — leads to the goal of **"making democracy safe for
capitalism"**: shrink democratic checks so capital stays unconstrained. *The Straussian Moment* (early
2000s) proposes a surveillance "super-system" that "operates outside the checks and balances of
representative democracy." [[palantir]] is that idea built.

## The intellectual lineage (the "blueprint")
- **Curtis Yarvin** (a.k.a. *Mencius Moldbug*, from 2007): democracy is chaos/tyranny; media + universities
  form a progressive "**Cathedral**"; replace democracy with a **CEO-monarch** ("national CEO," a tech-genius
  king with no limits). Neoreaction / "**NRx**."
- **Nick Land** ("**The Dark Enlightenment**," 2012): builds on Yarvin — ultra-capitalist **neo-feudal**
  order, authoritarian governance for "stability and growth." Both also espoused explicit racial hierarchy.
- **Carl Schmitt** — the Nazi-era jurist Thiel cites in his later Antichrist lectures.

The essay's structural argument: **Yarvin + Land wrote the theory; Thiel had the money and the
infrastructure ([[palantir]], [[founders-fund]], a manufactured VP [[jd-vance]]) to enact it.**

## Why hold it next to the vault's AI-optimism
The vault mostly meets this power structure through hopeful framings — [[elon-musk-economist-2026|Musk's
"work becomes optional"]], [[the-culture]], [[post-scarcity-and-meaning]]. This concept is the **same
technology, opposite politics**: AI + data as instruments of *elite control* rather than *broad liberation*.
The source's counter-thesis (from creator "Philip"): a society that "worships money" would be worse than
imperfect democracy, and **you should no more want a state run by billionaires than one run by a church** —
he anchors it in Jefferson's church/state "wall of separation." A useful, load-bearing tension when
reasoning about who owns the AI/surveillance stack. See [[agent-security-risks]].
