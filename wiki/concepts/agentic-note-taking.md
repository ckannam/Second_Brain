# Agentic Note-Taking

Letting AI agents do the data entry for your notes instead of manual logging. In [[obsidian-vault-deep-dive-emai]], local terminal agents auto-update daily notes and a daily-log template (activities + time tracking) as you work, feeding an [[obsidian-dataview|Dataview]] dashboard. Removes the friction/willpower that makes people abandon journaling — the same friction-reduction principle as this vault's [[llm-wiki-pattern]] and [[environment-design]].
