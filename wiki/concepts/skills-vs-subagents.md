# Skills vs Sub-agents

When to use a [[claude-code-skills|skill]] vs a [[claude-code-subagents|sub-agent]]:
- **Skill** — packaged, reusable *instructions/capability* the main agent invokes in-context when triggered. Best for a repeatable procedure.
- **Sub-agent** — a *separate delegated agent* with its own context window. Best for isolating a heavy or independent task.

Both are covered in [[master-claude-code-skills-28min]] and [[build-sell-claude-code-course]]. Scale further with [[claude-code-agent-teams]].
