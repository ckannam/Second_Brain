# Zero-Risk Offer

The trust-barrier hack behind the vault's [[selling-ai-automations|sales playbooks]]: do the work **free** in exchange for a case-study reference. The free client becomes the first paying client, and the resulting social proof compounds (nearly doubled reply rates for [[suvam]]). Also the "tiny free pilot" in [[christian]]'s 7-day plan ([[sign-first-ai-client-7-days]]). Core principle: **sell the outcome first, build after commitment.** Related: [[cold-email-outreach]].
