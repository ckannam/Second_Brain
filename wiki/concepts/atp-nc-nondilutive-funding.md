# ATP — NC State & Duke Non-Dilutive Funding (the state lane)

**Audience:** ATP internal strategy (Jaylen + team), with Cole running it down.
**Purpose:** map the **North Carolina state + Duke non-dilutive** capital ATP can reach — the lane
the federal map [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]] deliberately didn't cover. Built from the Nov 1
2026 memo ([[atp-nondilutive-funding-plan-2026-11]]) and **reconciled against ATP's own Drive**
(cost volume, Navy intake, opportunity tracker). Companion to [[athletic-training-portal]],
[[atp-competitive-analysis]], and the federal landscape page.

> **Why this is its own page.** [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]] owns the DoD/federal prize-and-SBIR
> lane (xTech, 711th HPW, SOCOM, NSF/NIH as federal R&D). This page owns the **state-triggered**
> money — the One NC programs that *piggyback on* a federal SBIR, NC IDEA, NCBiotech, and Duke I&E —
> plus the NC teaming/assistance orgs (NC-COMP, DEFTECH, SBTDC). The two pages meet at the SBIR:
> a federal Phase I proposal/award is what *unlocks* the One NC Incentive and Matching claims.

## 0. The move that actually matters first

**File the One NC Incentive claim on the pending Navy SBIR now (target Nov 15, 2026).** It is the
only NC money ATP can reach **without winning anything** — it reimburses a *submitted* proposal's
eligible costs. Durham is **Tier 3 → 50%**, capped at **$12K**, paid first-come from a ~**$210K**
pool that can run out. Two go/no-go checks before filing:
1. **NC Secretary-of-State registration timing** — ATP must have registered **before the federal
   submission, or ≥90 days before the claim.** ⚠️ *Open — confirm with Jaylen* (not in the Drive
   docs reviewed).
2. **Is the claim worth more than ~$1–2K?** At 50% it only pays out against *documented, paid*
   eligible costs — salaries actually paid (pay stubs + hour logs), invoices naming ATP, and the
   federal receipt notice. **Deferred/in-kind founder time does not count.** If ATP paid little
   cash during the proposal, the claim may be small.

Everything else on this page is a scheduled follow-on, not an immediate action.

## 1. The One NC pair (SBIR-triggered state money)

These are the highest-certainty items because they ride ATP's existing federal work.

| Program | Amount | Trigger | Key conditions | Status for ATP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One NC Small Business Incentive** | 50% Tier 3, cap **$12K** | A **submitted** federal SBIR/STTR proposal received in the FY2027 window (**Jul 1 2026 – Jun 30 2027**) | SOS registration (see §0); written **COI policy**; **≥51% of work in NC**; no overdue taxes; **one award/company/solicitation period** | **Likely eligible** — Navy **NV068** received **Sep 23, 2026** is inside the window; Durham Tier 3 ✅ |
| **One NC Matching Funds** | 50% up to **$75K** | An **executed** federal Phase I **award** | Needs the signed Phase I contract; ~**$1.92M** pool; FY2027 solicitation **releases Dec 14, 2026**; Tier-3 firms must not have >3 prior Match awards (ATP has 0 ✅) | **Downstream** — only after ATP *wins* a Phase I (Navy, or an xTech-finalist Army Phase I) |

**The one-award-per-solicitation-period trap.** The Incentive allows one claim per company per
solicitation period. The **Navy claim likely uses ATP's FY2027 slot**, so a second claim on an
**Army** Phase I proposal would need its federal receipt to fall **after Jul 1, 2027** (i.e., FY2028).
An xTech-finalist Army Phase I (submittable Mar 4–Apr 30, 2027) would land *inside* FY2027 → confirm
the limit before filing a second Incentive claim.

**💵 Drive-verified base for the match math:** ATP's Navy Phase I ceiling is **$315,000** (Base+Option;
ATP proposed **$314,168** per `ATP_Navy_SBIR_Vol3_Cost_Volume.xlsx`). If the Navy award lands at
Phase I, a 50%-up-to-$75K One NC Match is well within reach. See [[atp-nondilutive-funding-plan-2026-11]].

## 2. NC IDEA — MICRO over SEED (the full-time-founder question)

- **MICRO ($10K)** — $7.5K first tranche + $2.5K second; 8-week program; **15 awards/cycle**
  (~4% accept, Spring 2026: 336 apps). Eligibility: ≥1 founder living in NC + a plan to have a
  full-time NC founder within 6–12 months; prior investment/revenue **<$250K**. "Not a research
  grant" → pitch **milestones** (paid NCCU/Duke Athletics pilot conversions, validation vs. a
  gold-standard measurement), not a study.
- **SEED ($50K)** — **6 awards/cycle** (~3%); requires a **full-time NC founder at the time of
  applying**, <$250K revenue/investment, and market traction.
- **Only one of MICRO or SEED per cycle.** Spring 2027 dates unpublished; pattern is a **January
  info session + late-February deadline** (Spring 2026 closed Feb 23).

**The deciding variable is the full-time founder test, not the money.** Memo default: **MICRO**.
⚠️ **But reconcile:** ATP's Navy intake certifies **Jaylen has "primary employment with ATP"**
(SBIR PI rule). That isn't the same as NC IDEA's **40+ hrs/wk on startup, ≤15 hrs/wk school**, but
it means SEED may be live **if Jaylen genuinely clears that bar** — worth checking before conceding
to MICRO. ATP's ~$15K of non-dilutive money so far is well under the limits; funding is not the blocker.

## 5. Federal non-dilutive the memo adds (handoff to the federal page)

These extend [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]]; recorded here where the memo introduced them:

- **NSF SBIR/STTR** — Phase I ≤**$305K** (NSF 26-510), Phase II ≤$1.25M. Full-proposal dates
  **Jul 27 2026 / Nov 4 2026 / Mar 4 2027** (memo corrects Jaylen's "Mar 3"). **Requires a Project
  Pitch invite first (1–2 mo response)** → submit the pitch ~**January** to keep Mar 4 live, but
  don't force it; the realistic target is the **Jul 2027** window. Framing must be **deep-tech
  research with real technical risk** (single-camera 3D kinematics, uncertainty-aware injury-risk
  modeling), not product-only.
- **NIH SBIR (PA-27-100)** — due **Jan 5 & Apr 5, 2027**; Phase I ~$323K baseline (some institutes
  $400–700K). Medium fit via a clinical injury-prevention/rehab framing (NIAMS-type); needs a
  **clinical co-investigator (Duke Sports Medicine)** + human-subjects plan. Realistic target Apr 2027.
- **NC-COMP (RTI-led)** — 80+ org consortium ("prevent injury, speed recovery" for the military);
  **Duke + First Flight Venture Center are founding members.** Not a grant — a **teaming route** into
  DoD human-performance prototypes (e.g. MTEC Warfighter Brain Health). **Fit: high — ask to join as
  an industry member.** Ties to the MTEC/CDMRP items in [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]].
- **NC DEFTECH (NC Military Business Center)** — free state help with DoD SBIR/STTR/OTA; at
  Fayetteville Tech near Fort Bragg. Use to prep xTech and find Fort Bragg human-performance
  contacts. **Fit: high, no deadline.**
- **SBTDC** — free technology-commercialization counseling + One NC/SBIR guidance. **Book before
  NSF/Army proposals.**

Lower-priority adds the memo lists: American Underground (Durham coworking/FCAT fellowship —
networking, not money); Comcast NBCUniversal SportsTech (equity accelerator, later-stage, low
priority). Not verified this cycle: NCInnovation, SBA Growth Accelerator Fund, Durham city/county
grants, Duke CTSI pilots, NFL/NBA programs.
