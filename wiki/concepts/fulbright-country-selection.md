# Fulbright country selection (India / Brazil / Indonesia)

The live **decision point** in Cole's [[fulbright-us-student-program|Fulbright]] application: Fulbright wants **one focused country-specific proposal**, not a comparative study — and you only compete against applicants to your **same country + same award type**, so country choice is strategic (award rates vary a lot). Cole commits to one once host responses come in.

## Shortlist

Award rates below are **verified across two cloud passes (2026-07-29 + 2026-07-30)** against IIE 2021–2024 data via ProFellow, WorldPopulationReview, ScholarshipUnion, and official Fulbright country pages.

| Country | Award rate | Open Study/Research awards | Host / angle | Language to apply |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **India** — *top choice* | **~46%** ✅ *verified* (avg 2021–24; ~34% most recent cycle) | avg **55** grants/yr (2nd-most, after Germany's 91) | **IIM Ahmedabad** — Prof. **Pankaj Setia**, Centre for Digital Transformation; *or* **CIIE.CO / IIMA Ventures** (startup incubator running the **Bharat Inclusion Initiative**) | English fine; Hindi not required (**CLEA** option) |
| **Brazil** | **~64%** ✅ *verified — highest of any top-20 destination* | **30** Open Study/Research awards | **Porto Digital, Recife** — AI startup hub in **Northeast Brazil**, outside São Paulo/Rio/Salvador (which draw lower Fulbright priority) → stronger "underserved region" narrative | English fine; Portuguese not required (**CLEA** option) |
| **Indonesia** | **~24%** ⚠️ *corrected down from "~40%+"* (still > the ~20% program avg) | **13** Open Study/Research awards | **BRIN** (national research agency); **AMINEF** is the Fulbright Commission that reviews/selects and places grantees | English fine; Bahasa **not required** but willingness expected; grant may include up to **3 mo intensive language + 9 mo research** |

> **Verification — 2026-07-29/30 (@cloud).** Rates confirmed against secondary aggregators citing **IIE 2021–2024** Study/Research data. IIE does not publish clean per-country per-cycle rates, so treat these as **recent multi-year averages, not a locked current-cycle number.** India ~46% and Brazil ~64% confirmed Cole's prior figures. **Indonesia was overstated:** the vault had "~40%+"; the corrected figure is **~24%** (still above the ~20% overall program average, but Brazil's odds edge is decisive). Program-wide the U.S. Student Program awards **~20%** of applicants; range is **~4% (UK) to ~46% (India)**. Sources: [[fulbright-country-selection#Sources|below]].

**Cole's cycle = 2027–2028.** Indonesia's **2027–28 competition is open** (grants may begin **Sept 1 2027 – May 31 2028**, a 10–12-mo project). Brazil's 2026–27 round is **closed**; the 2027–28 round is the relevant one. Confirm the exact per-country award counts on the official award pages when the 2027–28 listings post, since annual counts drift.

Each host gives access to real founders/ecosystems for the [[ai-lowering-entrepreneurship-barriers]] thesis.

**Current-cycle grant counts (Open Study/Research):** Brazil offers **30** awards (+10 ETA), Indonesia **13**, India **~55/yr on average** (2nd-highest of any country, after Germany). India, Brazil, and Indonesia all offer a **Critical Language Enhancement Award (CLEA)** for extra language study.

## Deprioritized (researched, ruled out)
- **UK** (~4%), **Singapore** (~8%), **Kenya** (~9%) — too competitive.
- **Germany** (~32–33%) — strong *only* if pivoting to a biotech/deep-tech AI angle and dropping the underserved-economy framing.

## Decision logic
- **Brazil** has the best raw odds (~64%) and the cleanest "underserved region" story via Recife.
- **India** is Cole's stated top choice — best-developed host lead (Setia / CIIE.CO) and a large, vivid AI-entrepreneurship story; solid ~46% odds.
- **Indonesia** is the backup — award rate verified at **~24%** (below India/Brazil, above the ~20% program average); lean on AMINEF for placement and note the built-in language-study runway.
- Practical trigger: **commit when a host says yes.** A warm host affiliation letter (encouraged, not mandatory) both settles the country and strengthens the app.
