# Fulbright country selection (India / Brazil / Indonesia)

The live **decision point** in Cole's [[fulbright-us-student-program|Fulbright]] application: Fulbright wants **one focused country-specific proposal**, not a comparative study — and you only compete against applicants to your **same country + same award type**, so country choice is strategic (award rates vary a lot). Cole commits to one once host responses come in.

## Shortlist

| Country | Award rate | Host / angle | Language to apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **India** — *top choice* | ~46% | **IIM Ahmedabad** — Prof. **Pankaj Setia**, Centre for Digital Transformation; *or* **CIIE.CO / IIMA Ventures** (startup incubator running the **Bharat Inclusion Initiative**) | English fine; Hindi not required |
| **Brazil** | ~64% (highest of any major destination) | **Porto Digital, Recife** — AI startup hub in **Northeast Brazil**, outside São Paulo/Rio/Salvador (which draw lower Fulbright priority) → stronger "underserved region" narrative | English fine; Portuguese not required (**CLEA** = pre-grant language study option) |
| **Indonesia** | historically ~40%+ (verify current cycle) | **BRIN** (national research agency); **AMINEF** is the Fulbright Commission that places grantees with hosts once selected | English fine; Bahasa preferred, not required |

Each host gives access to real founders/ecosystems for the [[ai-lowering-entrepreneurship-barriers]] thesis.

## Deprioritized (researched, ruled out)
- **UK** (~4%), **Singapore** (~8%), **Kenya** (~9%) — too competitive.
- **Germany** (~32–33%) — strong *only* if pivoting to a biotech/deep-tech AI angle and dropping the underserved-economy framing.

## Decision logic
- **Brazil** has the best raw odds (~64%) and the cleanest "underserved region" story via Recife.
- **India** is Cole's stated top choice — best-developed host lead (Setia / CIIE.CO) and a large, vivid AI-entrepreneurship story; solid ~46% odds.
- **Indonesia** is the backup; verify the current-cycle award rate and lean on AMINEF for placement.
- Practical trigger: **commit when a host says yes.** A warm host affiliation letter (encouraged, not mandatory) both settles the country and strengthens the app.
