# Fulbright country selection (India / Brazil / Indonesia)

The live **decision point** in Cole's [[fulbright-us-student-program|Fulbright]] application: Fulbright wants **one focused country-specific proposal**, not a comparative study — and you only compete against applicants to your **same country + same award type**, so country choice is strategic (award rates vary a lot). Cole commits to one once host responses come in.

## Shortlist

Award rates below are **verified against the current cycle 2026-07-29** (see [[#Award-rate verification (2026-07-29)]] for sources and the one correction).

| Country | Award rate | Host / angle | Language to apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **India** — *top choice* | ~46% avg (2021–24); **~34% most recent cycle** | **IIM Ahmedabad** — Prof. **Pankaj Setia**, Centre for Digital Transformation; *or* **CIIE.CO / IIMA Ventures** (startup incubator running the **Bharat Inclusion Initiative**) | English fine; Hindi not required (**CLEA** = pre-grant language study option) |
| **Brazil** | ~64% (highest average of any major destination) | **Porto Digital, Recife** — AI startup hub in **Northeast Brazil**, outside São Paulo/Rio/Salvador (which draw lower Fulbright priority) → stronger "underserved region" narrative | English fine; Portuguese not required (**CLEA** = pre-grant language study option) |
| **Indonesia** | **~18% (≈1 in 5), 2021–24 avg** — *corrected down from the prior "~40%+" estimate* | **BRIN** (national research agency); **AMINEF** is the Fulbright Commission that places grantees with hosts once selected | English fine; Bahasa preferred, not required (**CLEA** = pre-grant language study option) |

Each host gives access to real founders/ecosystems for the [[ai-lowering-entrepreneurship-barriers]] thesis.

**Current-cycle grant counts (Open Study/Research):** Brazil offers **30** awards (+10 ETA), Indonesia **13**, India **~55/yr on average** (2nd-highest of any country, after Germany). India, Brazil, and Indonesia all offer a **Critical Language Enhancement Award (CLEA)** for extra language study.

## Deprioritized (researched, ruled out)
- **UK** (~4%), **Singapore** (~8%), **Kenya** (~9%) — too competitive.
- **Germany** (~32–33%) — strong *only* if pivoting to a biotech/deep-tech AI angle and dropping the underserved-economy framing.

## Decision logic
- **Brazil** has the best raw odds (~64%) and the cleanest "underserved region" story via Recife.
- **India** is Cole's stated top choice — best-developed host lead (Setia / CIIE.CO) and a large, vivid AI-entrepreneurship story; solid ~46% odds.
- **Indonesia** is the backup — but the odds are **weaker than Cole thought: ~18%, not ~40%+** (see verification below). That's below India and less than a third of Brazil's, so it stays third of three; lean on AMINEF for placement if pursued.
- Practical trigger: **commit when a host says yes.** A warm host affiliation letter (encouraged, not mandatory) both settles the country and strengthens the app.

## Award-rate verification (2026-07-29)

Verified Cole's prior numbers against current-cycle sources before he commits a country (the "@cloud verify" task). Study/Research grant, US Student Program, using ProFellow's compilation of the official 2021–2024 Fulbright statistics and the official country award pages.

- **Brazil ~64% — CONFIRMED.** Highest average award rate of the top-20-by-volume destinations (range 4% UK → 64% Brazil). **30** Open Study/Research awards + 10 ETA for the cycle.
- **India ~46% — CONFIRMED as the multi-year average,** but with a **downward trend: the most recent cycle was ~34%.** ~120 applications/yr and ~55 grants/yr on average (2021–24) — 2nd-most grants of any country. Still comfortably above most destinations; treat ~46% as the ceiling, ~34% as the realistic recent figure.
- **Indonesia — CORRECTED.** The vault's "historically ~40%+" is **not supported** by current data: recent figures put Indonesia at **~18% (≈1 in 5), 2021–24 average** — it is *not* in the top-20 highest-award-rate set (which starts at ~25%). **13** Open Study/Research awards. Old "~40%+" claim marked **superseded**. *(This one figure came from a search-engine summary of ProFellow/AMINEF, not a directly-fetched primary page — the direction is solid, but re-confirm the exact number on the official Indonesia award page before relying on it.)*
- **Deprioritized set re-checked:** UK ~4% ✓, Germany ~32% ✓ — both match the page's existing "ruled out" numbers.

**Bottom line for the decision:** Brazil's raw-odds advantage is even bigger than it looked, since Indonesia (the presumed easy backup) is actually the *hardest* of the three. Country ranking stands: Brazil (best odds) / India (top choice, still strong) / Indonesia (weak backup).
