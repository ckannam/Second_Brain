# Palantir

Data-integration / analytics company co-founded by [[peter-thiel]] (~2003), CEO **Alex Karp** (another
non-technical philosopher). Turns petabyte-scale, heterogeneous data into human-searchable models —
originally built from **PayPal's anti-fraud algorithms**. Source: [[antichrist-story-peter-thiel]].

## What it is
A **privatized intelligence layer**. Not consumer software — it's the tooling that lets a single
organization make sense of oceans of records. Early proof point: helped the SEC build the **Bernie
Madoff** case (40 yrs of records / 20+ TB). Grew via police "predictive policing" (LAPD, New Orleans,
NYPD) and then the full US security apparatus.

## Reach (per source, ~end 2025)
- Valuation **> $400B**; Thiel had to seed it with **$30M of his own money** after VCs (incl. Sequoia's
  Moritz) passed.
- Contracts with **every US three-letter agency + every military branch** (CIA, NSA, FBI, DHS, Marines,
  Air Force); NSA **XKeyscore** data; **ICE** deportations via **"ImmigrationOS"**; proposed linking
  **IRS + Social Security + visa** data into one queryable database.
- **IDF** targeting partnership (AI targeting / "Lavender"), controversial after Gaza aid-worker deaths.

## Why it's the hinge of Thiel's story
It's where his worldview flipped from **libertarian** (tech that frees individuals) to
**[[tech-authoritarianism|techno-authoritarian]]** (tech that lets an elite watch and control) — a
"surveillance super-system that operates outside the checks and balances of representative democracy"
(*The Straussian Moment*). The vault's [[agent-security-risks]] / surveillance concerns at civilizational
scale.
