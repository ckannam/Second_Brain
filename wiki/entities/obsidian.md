# Obsidian

Free markdown editor/organizer used as the **reading and browsing front end** for this
wiki. This vault is an Obsidian vault. Per [[andrej-karpathy]], Obsidian is the "IDE" —
you follow wikilinks, watch the graph view, and read LLM-generated pages in real time
while the agent edits them.

Relevant features: wikilinks (`[[page]]`), graph view (shows hubs and orphans), the
[[obsidian-web-clipper]] for capturing sources, and plugins like Dataview and Marp.

Sources: [[llm-wiki-karpathy]], [[build-an-ai-second-brain-matt-wolfe]].
