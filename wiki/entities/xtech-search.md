# xTech|Search (Army Prize Competition)

**xTech|Search** is the U.S. Army's flagship open-topic prize competition for small
businesses, run by the Army Applications Laboratory (AAL). As of 2026 it is in its 10th
iteration (xTech|Search 10). It pairs prize money with a direct path to Army SBIR Phase I
funding — making it one of the fastest routes from zero to a DoD contract for a
non-dilutive-funded company.

## What it is

- **Type:** Army prize competition (not a grant or contract). Winners receive cash awards;
  *finalists* (top ≤20 of ≤50 semifinalists) may separately file an Army SBIR/STTR Phase I.
- **Scope:** Open-topic. Companies self-describe why their solution is best-in-class and map
  it to Army needs. No prescribed list of tech areas per entry point, though the Army
  provides focus guidance (historically: force readiness, soldier support, autonomy,
  AI/ML, advanced materials, human-machine interface).
- **Operator:** Army Applications Laboratory (AAL), under Army Futures Command.
- **Portal:** xtech.army.mil — this is the submission portal, **not** DSIP.
- **Cadence:** Flagship runs ~annually. Topic-specific xTech competitions (e.g.
  xTech|Kinetic Reach, xTech|Soldier Lethality) run throughout the year.

Phase I. This is unlike the standard DSIP process — submitting a white paper to xTechSearch
is the prerequisite, not optional.

## Eligibility requirements

- For-profit **US small business** (≤500 employees)
- **>50% owned and controlled by US citizens or permanent residents**
  ⚠️ VC-backed companies: confirm this test still holds after each funding round — the
  ownership-and-control requirement is the single most common disqualifier.
- **One submission per company** per cycle.
- Disclose any federally funded *or pending* work substantially similar to the submission —
  the "substantially the same" rule explicitly covers *pending* proposals, not just awarded
  work. (Relevant for [[athletic-training-portal]]: the Navy "Aircrew Readiness Fingerprint"
  proposal must be disclosed and differentiated.)

## Army exclusion zones to know

xTechSearch *excludes* tech sitting **exclusively** in the Army Medical Research &
Development Command (USAMRDC) portfolio — i.e., clinical/rehabilitative medicine and
military operational medicine. This is the reason [[athletic-training-portal]] must frame
itself as **unit readiness / training-load / commander-facing availability monitoring**, not
as an injury-diagnosis or rehab tool. The reframe is the same one that keeps ATP on the
right side of FDA device risk ([[atp-competitive-analysis]]).

## Relation to DoD SBIR/STTR

xTech is **not** part of the monthly DSIP release cycle. It runs on its own timeline and
portal (xtech.army.mil). Winning or reaching finalist status in xTechSearch is one of the
legitimate alternative pathways to Army SBIR Phase I — the other is the standard DSIP
monthly pre-release. These paths are **additive**, not exclusive.
