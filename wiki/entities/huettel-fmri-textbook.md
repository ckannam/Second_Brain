# Huettel, Song & McCarthy — *Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging* (3rd ed.)

The **course textbook** for [[fmri]] (Functional Neuroimaging).

- **Authors:** Scott A. Huettel, Allen W. Song, Gregory McCarthy — all **Duke** (Huettel chairs Duke
  Psychology & Neuroscience). **3rd edition, 2014, Sinauer Associates.**
- Available at the Duke Bookstore and online. Readings are **dense and long** — the syllabus
  repeatedly warns to read *before* class and pair up with a study partner.

- **Ch 10 & 11** — Basic & Advanced Statistical Analyses → [[fmri-glm-analysis]] (Weeks 9–10).
