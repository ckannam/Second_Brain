# FastForward (JHTV's accelerator)

[[jhtv|JHTV]]'s startup **accelerator / incubator** — a coordinated suite of resources moving
Hopkins technologies from startup to market. **150+ companies** formed.

## What it provides
- **Startup resources:** legal & accounting support, fundraising assistance, networking, info resources.
- **Funding help:** identifying/securing capital at every stage — from non-dilutive
  [[translational-funding|translational grants]] → seed → Series A.
- **Space:** ~43,000 sq ft of affordable **incubator hubs** across Baltimore.
- Director of Technology Incubation & Acceleration (contact: jkagen@jhu.edu).
- Recent portfolio milestone: **Clasp Therapeutics** launched with **$150M** (precision immuno-oncology).

Part of the [[jhtv]] ecosystem Cole works in ([[capital-strategy]]).
