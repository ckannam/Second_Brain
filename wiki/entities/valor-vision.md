# Valor Vision (Valor Biomechanics)

**ATP's single closest direct competitor** — surfaced in the Oct 2026 refresh of
[[atp-competitive-analysis]] (it was missing from the Sept cut). Markerless 3D biomechanics from a
**single iPhone/iPad**: <5-minute assessment, movement scores + **30+ kinematic data points**,
asymmetry / injury-risk flags from baseline through return-to-play. An **optional Valor IMU sensor**
can be fused with the camera capture.

**Why it matters to ATP:** same capture mechanism (camera → pose → joint angles), same **college
buyer**, same **FERPA pitch** — but **already executing** where ATP is pre-seed. It directly contests
ATP's "college-native + FERPA-first" wedge, turning it from a moat into table stakes. See the
head-to-head in [[atp-competitive-analysis]] §2a.

- **Traction:** claims **5K+ athletes, 35K+ movements, 21+ sports.** Live in Power-4 college athletics
  (**LSU, Arizona State, TCU, Gonzaga, Tennessee**, UTampa, Houston Christian) + pro soccer (Juventus,
  Fulham, Southampton, Austin FC, Nashville SC) + the Parramatta Eels; selected for the Juventus
  Forward '25–'26 cohort.
- **Pricing (public):** Coach Base $89/coach/mo (≤100 athletes), Coach Pro $149/coach/mo (unlimited),
  Enterprise custom; athlete tiers free / $20 / $200. **ATP has no published price to answer this.**
- **Compliance:** advertises **FERPA, HIPAA BAA, GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2 / ISO 27001.**
- **Evidence:** **IMU vs. Vicon ±3.5°** (Sensors, 2024) — validates the *sensor*, not the camera
  pipeline. Still a partial head start on ATP (which has nothing published).
- **Funding:** lightly funded, **~$400K–$535K** across 3 rounds incl. a **$0.28M NSF grant (Sep 2023)**
  (CB Insights / Caplight). Founded **2021**; HQ **McLean, VA** (per site) / **Austin, TX** (per databases).

**Where ATP can still win vs. Valor:** truly **single-camera with no IMU dependency**; the
**longitudinal individualized baseline** (compounding per-athlete data vs. one-off scores); the
**DoD / aircrew second front** Valor doesn't touch ([[atp-federal-funding-landscape]]); and being
**first to a prospective, externally-validated study** (absent across the whole field, Valor's camera
pipeline included). ATP also needs a **published price**.
