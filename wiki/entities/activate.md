# Activate (activate.org)

A **national nonprofit** that turns PhD scientists/engineers into hard-tech founders via the
**Activate Fellowship** — a **two-year, non-dilutive, equity-free** program. The independent,
founder-first cousin of the [[jhtv|JHTV]] / [[fast-forward|FastForward]] model Cole works inside.
Cole's contact there: **[[Natasha Feshbach]]** (Chief of Staff, Fellowship — rose through fellowship
ops, ran the NY community). Appears **affiliated with the Fannie & John Hertz Foundation**.

## The fellowship
- **$300K+ over 2 years** (living stipend + R&D/expenses) + **~$100K** research funding / lab access
  per project. **No equity, no fees, no IP taken** — fellows keep full ownership.
- **~50 scientists/yr**; **PhD required**; **concept → early prototype** stage (pre-commercial), often
  pre-company. Broad hard tech (energy, robotics, semiconductors, aerospace, climate, some biotech).
- **Five communities:** Anywhere (NSF), Berkeley (Cyclotron Road / Lawrence Berkeley Lab), Boston,
  Houston (energy), New York (NYSERDA/Columbia, carbontech). Plus Global Singapore + JP/LA bridges.
- **Origin:** began as **Cyclotron Road**, embedded in a **DOE national lab** — energy/climate roots,
  now **"Hard Tech for Good."** Funded by **government + philanthropy** (NSF/DOE/NYSERDA/foundations).
- **Scale:** **346 scientists → 276 companies** to date.
- **People:** **[[Natasha Feshbach]]** — Chief of Staff, Fellowship (Cole's contact). Chief Fellowship
  Officer **Dan Recht**; Managing Directors per community (Jill Fuss–Berkeley, Josh Martin–Boston,
  Kevin See–Anywhere, Jeremy Pitts–Houston, Cristina Escoda–NY). [[Caitlin Cutter]] = Chief of Staff to
  the CEO (company-side, distinct from Natasha's fellowship-side COS).

## How it compares to JHTV (Cole's lens)
Same lab→market mission and same **[[translational-funding|non-dilutive]]** world Cole maps — but
Activate **is** the funder+program (JHTV *matches external* funders to spinouts), is **national/
cross-institution** (JHTV = Hopkins-only), takes **pre-company individual scientists** (JHTV = formed
spinouts), and is **founder-first on IP/equity** (university TTOs license IP + take equity). Full
side-by-side in **[[Caitlin Cutter|the prep brief]]**.
