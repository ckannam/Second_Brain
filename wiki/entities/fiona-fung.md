# Fiona Fung

Leader of **engineering and product for [[claude-code]] and [[claude-cowork]]** at
[[anthropic]]. Previously built and led teams at **Meta** and **Microsoft** (worked on
Visual Studio in the early 2000s).

In [[running-ai-native-engineering-org]] she lays out how the bottlenecks moved once agentic
coding became the org-wide default — see [[ai-native-engineering-org]] — and the team norms
Anthropic rewrote (planning, review, ownership, hiring, org shape). Signature lines:
"building is cheap, arguing is expensive"; "what served you prior may no longer."
