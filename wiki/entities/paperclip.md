# Paperclip

Free, open-source tool (paperclip.ing) for building and running an **entire company of AI agents** — a CEO agent hires engineers and delegates.

## Key mechanics (from [[claude-code-paperclip-openclaw]])
- **Heartbeats** (agent activity cadence), **skills**, **routines**, and **budgets** working together.
- Build a company from scratch; hire QA agents; import company templates; manage secrets/API keys with [[claude-code]].
- Marketed as beating [[openclaw]].

Concept: [[ai-agent-company]], [[multi-agent-orchestration]]. Related: [[clawdbot]].
