# PayPal Mafia

The cohort of early **PayPal** employees/founders who, after the 2002 eBay acquisition ($1.5B), went on
to build an outsized share of Silicon Valley. Source: [[antichrist-story-peter-thiel]].

## The network
- [[peter-thiel]] — PayPal CEO; later [[founders-fund]], [[palantir]], first Facebook investor.
- [[elon-musk]] — his **x.com** merged with Confinity to form PayPal; later Tesla / SpaceX / Neuralink.
- **Reid Hoffman** — LinkedIn.
- **Max Levchin** — co-founder; Affirm (popularized buy-now-pay-later).
- Ex-PayPal engineers/designers → founded **YouTube** (sold to Google), **Yelp**, a **Reddit** CEO.
- **Sam Altman** ([[openai]]) is named as a Thiel **mentee** in the broader orbit.

## Why it's a vault entity
It's the **origin graph** for people the vault meets separately — most directly [[elon-musk]] and, via
[[founders-fund]], [[openai]]. Explains why one small 1999 startup's alumni recur across the AI, space,
fintech, and social frontier. The unusual trait the source flags: the *diversity* of what they built
(networking, video, cars, rockets, payments) and the *hit rate*.
