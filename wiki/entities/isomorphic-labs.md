# Isomorphic Labs

The **scale leader** of [[techbio-ai-drug-discovery|AI drug discovery]] — the canonical
"AI-first platform" proof point in Cole's category POV for VC interviews
([[vc-interview-prep]], [[vc-investor-applications]]). This page gives it its own entity
node so the two named leaders in [[techbio-ai-drug-discovery]] (Isomorphic = scale, Insilico
= proof) are both linkable, not just referenced in prose.

## What it is
An **Alphabet / [[deepmind|DeepMind]] spinout**, founded **2021** in London, built to
"reimagine the entire drug-discovery process from first principles with an AI-first
approach." It commercializes the science behind **AlphaFold** — the protein-structure model
whose creators (incl. Demis Hassabis) won the **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry**.

- **CEO: Demis Hassabis** (also DeepMind CEO) — the founder-signal that lets Isomorphic
  raise and sign deals at a scale peers can't.
- **Model = platform + own pipeline.** It licenses its design platform to pharma **and**
  runs proprietary internal programs. Notably **AI-first with no wet lab of its own** — it
  partners for the physical chemistry/biology, keeping the moat in the models. This is the
  cleanest example of the "software-margin" thesis in a field that is otherwise capital- and
  lab-heavy.

## The technology (why it's the scale leader)
- **IsoDDE** (Isomorphic Drug Design Engine) — its post-AlphaFold design stack. A **Feb 2026**
  technical report reported **~50% accuracy on the hardest protein–ligand structure-prediction
  benchmarks vs ~23.3% for AlphaFold 3** — i.e. it has moved materially beyond the public model
  it was born from.
- Positioning: not "we predict structures" (AlphaFold did that) but "we **design molecules**
  against them" — the step that turns structure prediction into drugs.

## Funding & deals (the numbers that make the "scale" case)
- **~$600M** first external round (2024), then a **$2.1B Series B (May 2026)** led by
  **Thrive Capital** with GV, CapitalG, MGX (Abu Dhabi), Temasek (Singapore) and the UK
  Sovereign AI Fund → **~$2.7B total** outside capital in ~14 months. The **largest raise yet
  in AI drug discovery.**
- **Pharma partnerships: Eli Lilly (~$1.75B, 2024) and Novartis** → **~$3B in combined
  committed milestones** — validation that big pharma will pay for the platform, not just build
  in-house. (The Lilly deal is often paired against **Lilly–[[insilico-medicine|Insilico]]
  up to $2.75B** as the two headline AI-era pharma deals.)
- Secondary coverage has reported valuations **around $20B+**; treat the exact figure as
  approximate (funding totals and deal values are the firmer numbers).

## Where it is on the timeline (the honest caveat)
- Multiple proprietary candidates in **late preclinical**; expects to file **≥1 IND before
  end of 2026.** First **clinical trials were pushed from end-2025 to end-2026** (Hassabis).
- So Isomorphic leads on **scale and platform quality**, but — consistent with
  [[techbio-ai-drug-discovery]]'s sharp nuance — **AI compresses discovery/preclinical, not
  the clinic**: it has **no human efficacy readout yet**, which is exactly why Insilico (Phase 2
  Rentosertib) is the *proof* leader while Isomorphic is the *scale* leader.

## Why Cole tracks it
The single best name-drop for the AI-drug-discovery category POV: it fuses the [[jhtv]]
[[valley-of-death]] lens (AI attacking the expensive front half of the pipeline) with a
software-style platform/margin story VCs recognize. Pair with the [[insilico-medicine]] proof
point for a balanced "scale vs. proof" framing in interviews.
