# SpaceRep (spacerep.app)

A commercial **[[spaced-repetition]] app** — the closest existing competitor to Cole's
[[neuro-channel|learning-app]] idea, and useful market validation for it. Source: web clip
`raw/Processed/SpaceRep - Remember Everything You Learn.md` + the live site.

## What it is
- **Positioning:** *"Spaced Repetition That Works With Your Calendar."* Its whole differentiator is
  **Google Calendar integration** — it scans your calendar for free slots and auto-schedules
  15–20 min review sessions around your life, "conflict-aware."
- **Engine:** "Advanced **FSRS** algorithm" (the open, commodity scheduler — see
  [[spaced-repetition]]). Imports standard Anki `.apkg` decks.
- **Features:** dynamic flashcards, progress/retention analytics.
- **Pricing:** Hobby **$7/mo** (limited decks); Pro **$17/mo** (unlimited decks, "AI integration"
  — vaguely specified). ~30% annual discount. Explicitly targets students.

## The gap it reveals (why "we could do better" is real)
SpaceRep is **flashcards only**. It does **not**:
- triage material into [[knowledge-types|Skill / Concept / Fact]];
- coach **concepts** (no from-scratch explain-back, no analogy stress-test);
- generate **interleaved practice** for skills ([[interleaving]]);
- integrate with a knowledge base / notes ([[llm-wiki-pattern|vault]]).

Its moat is "it's in your calendar" — thin, and orthogonal to the science. The unoccupied
position the evidence points to is a **spacing engine that routes the right retrieval modality per
knowledge type** with an LLM doing what a flashcard can't ([[retrieval-practice]],
[[spacing-math-meta-analysis-murray-2025]]). That's the [[neuro-channel]] channel × app thesis;
FSRS/Calendar scheduling is table-stakes to match or skip, not the product.
