# Everyday Robotics

A **Johns Hopkins spinout** building **LLM-powered social robots**. First product: a tabletop
robot that helps **children learn to read**, deployed in **Baltimore schools**. Surfaced through
Cole's [[jhtv]] summer and picked as one of his two **[[dorm room fund]]** investment
recommendations (the JHU pick).

## Founders
- **[[Victor Nikhil Antony]]** — technical founder. **JHU computer science PhD** (B.S. CS,
  University of Rochester). Lead author on **ELLA** (Early Language Learning Agent), an
  autonomous, generative-AI social robot for early language development. Has **designed and open
  sourced a suite of socially assistive robots** used in real-world studies across education,
  health, and eldercare. **2026 O'Shaughnessy Fellowship**, lead program chair of the 2026 HRI
  Pioneers workshop, Baltimore Innovation Initiative award. Unusual origin: left traditional school
  at 14 for THINK Global School, studying across 14 countries.
- **Andrés de la Sierra Renier** — business founder. Ex-**J.P. Morgan** (and Eliasson Group);
  NYU Gallatin. The operator/finance complement to Antony's research side.

The pitch in one line: **a relentless technical builder paired with a real operator** — a
complete, complementary founding team, which is a strong early-stage signal.

## Traction & recognition
- Won JHU's **2026 President's Venture Fellowship and Blaze Award** (run through [[jhtv]] — the
  award Cole's summer team touches, which is how it came onto his radar).
- Real classroom deployments (not lab demos) generating interaction data no dataset can replicate.

## Why it's an investable DRF pick
- **Team over idea**, fits DRF's stated thesis ("a builder we knew would keep going").
- **Deep-need market:** only **~31% of Baltimore City students read at grade level (31.2% on the
  2024–25 MCAP), the lowest rate in Maryland** — high-need environments are where embodied AI can
  reach real-world adoption fastest, because the demand already exists.
- Personal angle for Cole: his **mom is a librarian at a Baltimore city public school**.
