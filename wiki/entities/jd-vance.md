# J.D. Vance

US Vice President; former venture capitalist and author of *Hillbilly Elegy* (2016). In
[[antichrist-story-peter-thiel]] he is the clearest case of [[peter-thiel]] **manufacturing a
politician**.

## The Thiel-built career (per source)
- Heard Thiel's 2011 Yale talk (higher-ed as a bubble); left law for **VC at Thiel's firms**.
- A self-described **"Never Trump guy"** who called Trump "cultural heroin" and a possible "America's
  Hitler" — then rebranded MAGA to run for Ohio Senate (2022).
- Thiel spent **~$15M** on a pro-Vance super-PAC (more than Vance's own campaign raised) and, as Trump's
  most prominent Silicon Valley ally, brokered the **Trump endorsement** that won the primary.
- The essay's claim: without Thiel's money + network there is no Senate seat and no vice-presidency —
  "he didn't just influence Vance's career, he created it." Thiel's stated payoff: an ally "a heartbeat
  away from the presidency," advancing [[tech-authoritarianism|capitalism-over-democracy]] aims.
