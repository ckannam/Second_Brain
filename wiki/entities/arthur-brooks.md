# Arthur Brooks

Social scientist and one of the best-known popularizers of the **science of human happiness**.
Professor at Vanderbilt University and senior fellow at Harvard Business School; CBS News
contributor, columnist at *The Free Press*, host of the podcast *Office Hours with Arthur Brooks*.
Author of 16 books, including the #1 NYT bestsellers *Build the Life You Want* (with Oprah Winfrey),
*From Strength to Strength*, and *The Meaning of Your Life: Finding Purpose in an Age of Emptiness*.

Former president of the American Enterprise Institute; his current work is squarely happiness/meaning,
grounded in behavioral science but explicitly starting from **philosophy and the wisdom tradition**
("the best questions come from philosophers and theologians, not behavioral scientists"). His favorite
philosopher is **Ralph Waldo Emerson** — he leans on *Self-Reliance* (1841) as the template for
breaking modern addictions: **be honest with yourself → think for yourself → go your own way.**
