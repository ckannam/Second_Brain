# Obsidian Dataview

Obsidian plugin that queries note frontmatter/properties to build tables, charts, and dashboards — turning the vault into a live dashboard. Central to the [[obsidian-vault-deep-dive-emai|Easy Machine AI system]], fed by AI-triaged daily-note properties. Related: [[obsidian]], [[agentic-note-taking]].
