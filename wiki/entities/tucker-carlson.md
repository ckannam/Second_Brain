# Tucker Carlson

American political commentator; former Fox News host, now runs his own media operation and a widely-heard
podcast. Once highly influential over Trump's movement, he has become — per the 2026
[[tucker-carlson-economist-2026|Economist interview]] — **MAGA's most prominent dissident** after breaking
with the White House over the **Iran war** ("Operation Epic Fury"), which he called "disgusting and evil."

## Positions on file (from [[tucker-carlson-economist-2026]])
- Iran war is a world-historic mistake; the real prize is **control of the Strait of Hormuz**, and the biggest
  winner is **Russia**.
- Heavy **foreign-lobby / campaign-finance** critique of US foreign policy; Gaza is "a disaster" and a moral
  horror; insists on **universal human rights, not ethnic rights** and refuses the "Zionist" label.
- Won't call **Ukraine** "a sovereign country"; blames the US government for provoking Russia via NATO
  expansion and sanctions; says the **US can't and won't defend Taiwan** and must accept **power-sharing with
  China**; still regards **Europe** as America's deepest ally.

A first-principles, contrarian, empire-skeptic worldview — recorded as *his stated positions*, not vault
endorsement.
