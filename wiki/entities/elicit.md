# Elicit

An AI **research assistant** company (scientific research, empirical / high-stakes
decision-making) that prides itself on reliability, high quality, and **data provenance**.
Presented by [[james-brady]] in [[trustworthy-agentic-workflows-dsl]].

Elicit's research agent is built around a custom [[agentic-dsl|domain-specific language]],
**AshPL** — a Turing-incomplete, typed subset of Python with domain primitives (retrieve
academic papers, clinical trials). The design encodes three values: a **legible** process,
**fidelity** under iteration, and **faithful** execution — a concrete case of
[[mechanism-over-output]]. Builds on [[claude-code|Claude]] models.

Related: [[anthropic]], [[rag]].
