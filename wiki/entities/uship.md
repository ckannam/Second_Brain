## What it is
A **summer move-out storage** service for college students — store boxes/furniture/appliances
between terms, with local truck rentals for move-out. Whale mascot; sign-up at
`universityshipping.com/signup`. **Duke-focused** customer base (`@duke.edu` students + their
affluent, often-Northeast parents). [[carson-uship-vault-page]]

- **Business model = storage-first franchise.** One software platform ([[uship-os]]) serves many
  campuses; each campus is run near-solo by a local **co-owner** who keeps a profit share while
  **HQ keeps the rest**. Profit split by campus: **Duke = HQ 0%** (founding team keeps 100%),
  **Columbia = 50/50**, **all new schools = 80/20**.
- **Storage-first vs. the shipping pitch.** Customer marketing headlines year-round storage
  *plus* shipping to/from home ("cheapest of any competitor"); the internal OS treats UShip as
  **storage-only with a small shipping add-on** (~20% Square charge on shipping items). The
  home-shipping line is a smaller Northeast offering, kept **legacy/off-platform** — new schools
  are storage-only. [[carson-uship-vault-page]]

## Email marketing — Cole's engine & the scraped-list moat
This was **Cole's domain**; he trained Carson on it (2026-06-30) and handed off the role.
- **Tooling:** customer email runs on **Wix email marketing**, off an inherited **template**
  iterated year-over-year (analytics say it works — don't stray far). **Cadence:** ~1×/week in
  summer, **2×/week (Tue/Thu)** in the school year, ramping hard before move-out/move-in.
  Signup confirmation emails are built into Wix.
- **The list ("2026 scrape"):** ~16k contacts, all Duke students on unique **Net-IDs — undergrad
  *and* grad** (via the DUID scrape), sub-segmentable by class year (~1,700 incoming freshmen as
  "class of 2029"; the freshman blast draws the most clicks, ~4,000).
- **Deliverability is the load-bearing rule:** send only to **accurate Net-ID emails** — bounces
  flag the sender as spam and tank reputability ("the biggest harm"). This is **what broke the
  Richmond expansion attempt** (no Net IDs → inaccurate scraped addresses → bounces). Bulk sends
  use `contact@` + **BCC-everyone** hygiene; `contact@universityshipping.com` re-routes to all
  six operators.
- **The list is treated as a competitive moat** — Cole rated it well above how peers
  ([[Bull City Beds]], Blue Box) gather emails. Original scrape credited to "Joe Zachaloys"
  (graduated); CSVs held by [[Matthew Moskow]]. A monetization idea (selling CSVs to BCB for
  ~$2k) was floated but is **legally unresolved**. [[carson-uship-vault-page]]
