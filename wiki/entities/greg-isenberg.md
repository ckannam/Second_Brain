# Greg Isenberg

Host of the **Startup Ideas Podcast** and CEO of Late Checkout (LCA) — builds product/idea businesses in the AI era. Made **[[autoresearch-broke-internet-greg-isenberg]]**, a solo primer on [[autoresearch]] plus **10 business ideas** built on top of it and coverage of Karpathy's **[[agent-hub]]** launch.

Angle: opportunity-spotting — "when you see [[andrej-karpathy]] doing something like this, pay attention, tinker, find where the opportunity is in the fog." Frames autoresearch as a democratized version of what frontier labs already do, and stresses keeping a human in the loop for high-stakes uses (e.g. trading, medicine). Runs ideabrowser.com.

Covered the autoresearch wave alongside [[david-andre]] and [[nick-saraev]].
