# Duke Capital Partners (DCP)

Duke's **official early-stage venture arm** — the *capital* pillar of the
[[duke-innovation-ecosystem]]. **Formerly the Duke Angel Network** (est. 2015); rebranded to Duke
Capital Partners. Runs an **angel-investing platform + a co-investment fund** (the **Duke
Innovation Fund**) that leverage the Duke alumni network.

## By the numbers
- Evaluates **~1,000 startups/year** → deep DD on ~**200** → invests in **12–24**.
- **$105M+ deployed** across **60+** early-stage companies; typical checks **$1–5M**, U.S. startups
  ~2–3 yrs old.
- Top sectors: **AI (9), SaaS (9), Health Care (9), Medical Devices (8)**; portfolio incl.
  **Otter.ai, restor3d, Vestwell.** A **top-5 university investment entity in North America.**

## Why it matters for the comparison
This is where Duke's "capital function" lives — an **affiliated angel/fund network**, *not* a staff
capital-strategy team embedded in tech transfer like [[capital-strategy|JHTV's]]. Structural
contrast is the crux of the [[kelli-luginbuhl-call-prep|Kelli conversation]]. Related:
[[duke-otc]] · [[duke-innovation-ecosystem]] · [[jhtv]].
