# Founders Fund

Venture-capital firm founded by [[peter-thiel]] (~2005), portfolio reportedly ~$20B. The capital node
behind a large share of the frontier the vault tracks. Source: [[antichrist-story-peter-thiel]].

## Why it matters here
It is an **early investor in [[openai]]**, **SpaceX**, **Neuralink**, **DeepMind**, **Stripe**,
**Airbnb**, **Spotify**, **Figma**, **Affirm**, **Palantir**, and more — i.e. much of the AI + defense
frontier. When the vault reads [[elon-musk]]'s SpaceX/Neuralink or OpenAI's models, Thiel's fund is
often upstream of the money.

## The shift the source traces
Early Founders Fund investments were framed as **freedom-expanding decentralization** (Airbnb vs hotels,
Lyft vs taxis, Facebook vs gatekept media). Through the 2010s the emphasis moved to **surveillance and
war**: a **$1B** bet on **Anduril** (AI border/military towers) — its largest ever — plus European
attack-drone / remote-warfare startups. The client shifted from "individuals seeking freedom online" to
**the government**. Mirrors Thiel's own [[tech-authoritarianism|libertarian → authoritarian]] arc.
