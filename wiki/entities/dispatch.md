# Dispatch

A way to control [[claude-code]] remotely from your phone. Paired with [[claude-code-computer-use]] to drive a Mac remotely ([[claude-code-computer-use]]). One of the remote-access options alongside [[claude-code-channels|Channels]] and [[claude-code-remote-control|Remote Control]] ([[claude-code-imessage]]).
