# Claude Design

An [[anthropic|Anthropic]] design tool: describe what you want in **plain language** and get
**production-quality** design outputs that **ship in your brand**. Built by a small team
"from prompt to production" ([[designing-with-claude-prompt-to-production]]).

Extends [[natural-language-coding]] / [[vibecoding]] into visual design. Related:
[[claude-code]], [[html-over-markdown-specs]].
