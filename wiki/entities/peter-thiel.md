# Peter Thiel

Billionaire investor, PayPal co-founder, and the money+ideology behind a large slice of the tech
world this vault otherwise tracks only through its products. Born Frankfurt 1967; childhood in
apartheid-era **Swakopmund, Namibia**; Stanford philosophy + law. Primary source:
[[antichrist-story-peter-thiel]] (an openly critical video essay — treat motive claims as its argument).

## Why he matters to this vault
He is the **capital and worldview node** connecting entities the vault sees separately:
- Co-founded **PayPal** → the **[[paypal-mafia]]** (→ [[elon-musk]], Reid Hoffman/LinkedIn, YouTube, Yelp,
  Affirm, and mentee **Sam Altman** of [[openai]]).
- Founded **[[founders-fund]]**, an early investor in **[[openai]]**, SpaceX, Neuralink, DeepMind, Stripe,
  Anduril, Palantir — i.e. a backer of much of the current AI/defense frontier.
- Built **[[palantir]]**, now a >$400B privatized-surveillance backbone for US/allied governments.
- Created VP **[[jd-vance]]**'s career; endorsed Trump (2016) as leverage, not belief.

## The through-line (per the source)
A **libertarian → authoritarian** arc. Early Thiel framed technology as *decentralizing power to
individuals* (PayPal as ungovernable money; Facebook/Airbnb/Lyft as dissolving institutions). After
**9/11** (surveillance opportunity) and the **2008 bailouts** (free market rescued by the state), he
concluded — in the essays ***The Straussian Moment*** and ***The Education of a Libertarian*** (2009) —
that **"freedom and democracy are [no longer] compatible."** His stated goal became **"making democracy
safe for capitalism"**: keep capitalism unconstrained by shrinking democratic checks. See
[[tech-authoritarianism]].

## Contradiction the source hammers
He now lectures on the **Antichrist** — casting progressivism, one-world government, and charity as
tools of a coming tyranny that will halt technological progress — while personally building the
surveillance and defense tech ([[palantir]], Anduril) such a regime would use. When confronted on the
hypocrisy on camera, per the essay, "he had no answer." The creator's read: the philosophy is a
rationalization; the constant is **accumulation of money and control**.
