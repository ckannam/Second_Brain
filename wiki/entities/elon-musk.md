# Elon Musk

Founder/CEO across **Tesla, SpaceX** (incl. **Starlink**), **xAI** (the **Grok** model), **Neuralink**
(brain–computer interfaces — the one venture that touches this vault's [[neuroscience-of-behavior|neuro]]
cluster), **The Boring Company**, and owner of **X**. Self-described "instinctive builder." As of the 2026
[[elon-musk-economist-2026|Economist interview]], reportedly worth ~$1trn, interviewed just after SpaceX's IPO.

## Views on file (from [[elon-musk-economist-2026]] — his claims, not established fact)
- **Superintelligence timeline:** AI exceeds the sum of human intelligence in **~5 years (~2031)**; an "age of
  amazing abundance" follows where **"work is optional"** and **"money won't matter."**
- **Control:** humans probably won't be "in charge" in 10 years (the chimp analogy); best lever is AI that is
  **maximally truth-seeking and curious**. Holds ~10–20% catastrophic risk but has moved to "enjoy the ride."
- **Economy = digital + physical intelligence:** humanoid robots (**Optimus**) are the "endectors" that turn
  digital AI into physical output.
- **Industry:** calls **[[anthropic|Anthropic]]** "currently the leader in AI"; says OpenAI spun out of his
  attempt to counterweight Google, and Anthropic spun out of OpenAI. References the vault's model lineage —
  **[[fable-5|Fable]]** as smartest, **[[mythos|Mythos]]** as its predecessor — plus China's **Kimi K3**.
- **China AI race:** the binding constraint is **electricity** (China > US+Europe+India combined), not chips;
  export controls won't stop China leading eventually.
- **Politics:** says he "got too involved," defends the USAID cuts, and addresses Ukraine/Starlink, Europe, and
  accusations of racism in the same interview.

Note: Musk is a **primary actor** in the AI race this vault otherwise tracks through [[anthropic]] and the
Claude model pages — useful as an outside-the-Anthropic-bubble read, and a contrast to [[model-speciation]]
(he expects convergent superintelligence, not divergent niches).

**Cultural touchstone:** Musk cites **[[the-culture|Banks's Culture]] series** as the best depiction of the
future — an *optimistic* human-plus-superintelligence utopia — and named SpaceX drone ships after its
sentient starships. It's the fictional endpoint of his "abundance / work-optional" thesis
(→ [[post-scarcity-and-meaning]]).
