# 711th Human Performance Wing (711 HPW)

The **711th Human Performance Wing** is the U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory's
human-performance unit at **Wright-Patterson AFB** (Dayton, OH), with additional sites in
**San Antonio, TX** and **Mesa, AZ**. It is the Air Force's single organization for human-centric
research, aerospace medicine, and human-systems integration — and, for [[athletic-training-portal|ATP]],
the **highest-fit DoD customer** in the entire [[atp-federal-funding-landscape|federal funding landscape]].
Opening a relationship here is one of the three near-term federal moves that actually matter for ATP.

## What it is

- **Parent:** Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL). **Location:** Wright-Patterson AFB, OH (HQ) +
  San Antonio, TX + Mesa, AZ.
- **Activated 2008**, consolidating AFRL's **Human Effectiveness Directorate** with parts of the 311th
  Human Systems Wing (Brooks City-Base, TX) — including the School of Aerospace Medicine, the Air Force
  Institute for Operational Health, and the 311th Performance Enhancement Directorate. Billed as the
  first "human-centric warfare" wing to consolidate research, education, and consultation.
- **Mission:** discover, develop, and deliver human-performance knowledge and solutions — "exploit
  biological and cognitive S&T to optimize and protect the Airman's capability to fly, fight, and win in
  air, space, and cyberspace." Three mission areas: **aerospace medicine, science & technology, and
  human-systems integration.**
- **Leadership:** a change-of-command was held **March 30, 2026** (AFRL names Brig. Gen. Robert Bogart;
  older references list Brig. Gen. Dale Harrell — confirm the current commander before citing).

## Component directorates

| Directorate | Code | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Airman Systems Directorate** | **RH** | The research arm (formerly the *Human Effectiveness Directorate*). Biological/cognitive S&T, wearable sensing, human-machine teaming. Runs an **Airman Sensing & Assessment** product line (wearable physiological monitoring). |
| **U.S. Air Force School of Aerospace Medicine** | **USAFSAM** | Aerospace medicine education, research, and operational health. |
| **Human Systems Integration Directorate** | **HP** | Integrating the human into Air Force systems design. |

The old name **"Human Effectiveness Directorate"** still appears in pre-~2015 materials for what is now
RH — the same unit, renamed.

## Why it matters for ATP (the fit)

- **Aircrew readiness is exactly 711 HPW's lane — and exactly ATP's Navy angle.** ATP's pending Navy SBIR
  is the **"Aircrew Readiness Fingerprint"** (see [[atp-competitive-analysis]]); 711 HPW folds in USAFSAM
  and runs aircrew physiological-monitoring work (e.g. an Integrated Cockpit Sensing effort). ATP's
  single-camera markerless pose → joint-angle/baseline-deviation model is a readiness-monitoring tool,
  not a cold start in this room.
- **The MSK-injury readiness problem is the DoD's #1 human-performance cost**, and 711 HPW/RH's wearable
  sensing + human-machine-teaming portfolio is where that work lives on the Air Force side.
- **The wellness-safe reframe applies here too.** Position ATP as **unit-readiness / training-load /
  availability monitoring** (deviation from an individual's baseline), not diagnosis — the same reframe
  [[atp-competitive-analysis]] prescribes to stay off the FDA device line.
