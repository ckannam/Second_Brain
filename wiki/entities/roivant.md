# Roivant Sciences

**Roivant Sciences** (NASDAQ: ROIV) is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company founded
in 2014 that builds and runs a family of nimble drug-development subsidiaries it calls
**"Vants."** Rather than operating as one monolithic pharma, Roivant spins up focused Vants
to develop and commercialize a specific medicine or technology, then runs them in parallel.
Its stated mission is to "improve the lives of patients by accelerating the development and
commercialization of medicines that matter." Offices in **New York** (1 Penn Plaza) and
**Basel, Switzerland**. This is a **[[Job Search]] target** — the entry point Cole is
pursuing is the **Rotational Analyst Program** (below).

## The "Vant" model
- A **holding company** that incubates and operates specialized subsidiaries ("Vants"), each
  built around a promising asset or platform. The structure trades a single big org for many
  small, agile, accountable teams — the pharma analogue of a **portfolio of operating
  companies**, which is why the seat sits right at Cole's **science + capital + operator**
  intersection.
- Track record: **9 FDA approvals** and **12 positive Phase 3 trials since 2019**; strength
  in **dermatology and inflammatory/immunology** disease.

## Key programs (snapshot — verify before citing live)
- **VTAMA (tapinarof cream)** — FDA-approved 2022 for plaque psoriasis (later atopic
  dermatitis).
- **LISRAYA (brepocitinib)** — launched 2026 for adults with dermatomyositis.

## Rotational Analyst Program (Cole's target)
A **two-year** early-career program that develops future leaders across the Roivant family.
Analysts complete **four six-month rotations** across teams, functions, and the portfolio of
Vants — a fast route to broad exposure across drug development, strategy, and commercialization.
