# Ply (getply.com)

**NYC seed-stage vertical-SaaS startup** (founded 2022 by Dave Wigder & Darion Miller). Cloud
**inventory + purchasing software for the trades** — helps HVAC/R, plumbing, and electrical contractors
manage materials, purchasing, and stock across **warehouses, trucks, and job sites**. Tagline:
*"Materials Management Simplified."* Cole's contact there: **[[Ryan Berger]]** (Head of Operations).

- **Funding:** **~$14.2M** total. Pre-seed **$1.7M** (Cleo Capital); **$8.5M** led by **Ferguson
  Ventures** (corporate VC of Ferguson, a ~$29B plumbing/HVAC/waterworks distributor) with **Primary**
  and **SignalFire** participating. Ferguson = a **strategic** investor (distribution + catalog data +
  industry credibility; watch for channel-conflict/dependency tradeoffs).
- **Why it's relevant to Cole:** dead-center in his [[five-year-plan]] "**join an early NYC startup as
  an operator**" lane — though it's **construction-tech, off his health/bio tilt**. Strong Uship overlap
  (trucks / warehouses / units / invoicing) makes Cole a credible operator peer here.
