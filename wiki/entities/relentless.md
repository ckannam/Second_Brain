# Relentless (JoinRelentless)

**Relentless** (joinrelentless.com, "JoinRelentless") is a **done-for-you / managed job-search
service** — you outsource the grind of a job hunt to an expert team and "wake up to a calendar full of
interviews." Aimed mainly at **senior and executive professionals** (Director / VP / CTO level), though
testimonials span analysts to CEOs. This page is the vault's reference on the service — **Cole flagged it
specifically to answer questions about it later** (source clips ingested 2026-08-18).

> **Read as marketing.** Everything below is drawn from the company's own **testimonial videos** (posted
> to a "Relentless Reviews" channel, each ending in a `?vip_referral=rh` link). Outcomes are
> self-reported by satisfied customers — expect strong **selection/survivorship bias** and no downside
> cases. Treat the *what-they-do* as reliable and the *results* as promotional.

## What they actually do (the service)
A full-funnel, hands-off job search:
- **Resume rewrite + strategic positioning** — reframe experience for "the times"; **quantify impact**
  (especially for tech people who "don't take credit" the way salespeople do) and **name-drop
  recognizable brands** (e.g. NASA, Capital One).
- **Role/target mapping** — an exercise to clarify *which* roles you're actually going for and the key
  skills the resume must reflect.
- **Behind-the-scenes outreach** — the team emails **recruiters and hiring managers directly**,
  including high-level VP/Director outreach, to get you noticed past the ghosting.
- **Pitch coaching** — teaching short, confident messaging (a **two-sentence** email to a hiring
  manager) and how to deliver a succinct verbal pitch.
- **Interview prep + logistics** — a prep **document per role**, plus **calendar management** (they book
  and manage interviews, and can **link to your work calendar**).
- **Net effect they sell:** it "gets your head out of the job search," freeing mental capacity so you can
  keep working / run a business while the search happens in the background.

## Positioning claims
- **"Applying online is only ~10% of what you need to do"** — the due diligence (finding the right
  recruiters/hiring managers, direct outreach) is the other 90% and is what eats time.
- **Ghosting at the senior level is brutal** and the game has "changed beneath your feet" — a good resume
  you wrote yourself often just "doesn't read the way you think it reads."
- **ROI framing:** it only makes sense if the target role pays enough that the comp **increase** covers
  the cost — best fit is higher-level, higher-salary searches.
- **Best-fit customer:** someone **already employed** (less desperation, better leverage — "always look
  for a job when you have a role"), **post-layoff**, **burned out / time-poor**, or an **entrepreneur**
  running something on the side. Explicitly pitched as valuable **even while employed**.
