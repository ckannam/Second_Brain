# Dorm Room Fund

**Dorm Room Fund (DRF)** is a student-run pre-seed venture capital fund — the only fund whose
Investment Partners are exclusively current college students, writing first checks to other
student founders. Founded in 2012 as a program of [[First Round Capital]] and now operating as
an independent fund; active across ~30 partner campuses in the US.

## Organization

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Founded | 2012 (First Round Capital program → independent fund) |
| Fund size | $50M (2026 fund; ~4× the prior fund) |
| Partner campuses | ~30 (incl. Penn, Princeton, MIT, Stanford, Harvard, Columbia, CMU, Duke, JHU, …) |
| Investment Partners | ~60 students selected per year |
| Decision-making | 100% student — Investment Partners source, diligence, and invest capital |
| Check size | Pre-seed; first-check focus |
| Eligibility | Student founders only (must have ≥1 semester of school left at first check) |

## Model

DRF splits Investment Partners into **regional teams** (e.g., Philadelphia & Southeast covers
Duke + JHU). Each team meets weekly to source deals, do diligence, and vote on investments.
The fund writes first checks; follow-on is rare. Students commit ~10–15 hrs/week to the role.

The founding insight: **students best find student founders**. DRF's network effect compounds
across cohorts — alumni found companies, hire from the network, and return as mentors.

## Investment Partner role

Selection is highly competitive (~hundreds of applicants per region). The process:
1. Written application (six prompts: bio, investment, thesis, deal sourced, startup, DRF-specific)
2. Two rounds of interviews
3. Super Day (final round, cohort-wide)
4. Results ~1 week after Super Day
