# Claude Tag (@Claude)

A **proactive, multiplayer** form of Claude from [[anthropic]]: instead of opening Claude and
asking, you **tag @Claude** (in Slack, beta — claude.com/tag) and it **jumps in on its own**,
does work that can span **days or weeks**, follows up, and **remembers for next time**.
Because it lives in a channel, it's multiplayer — the whole team sees and participates.

Presented by [[boris-cherny]] and [[cat-wu]] in [[future-of-work-claude-tag]] as the next
step after [[claude-code]]: person-in-the-loop → one person + ~10 Claudes → a whole team
collaborating with a Claude that "drives." Built on long-horizon + alignment research and a
memory system that "finally feels right." A concrete [[proactive-agents|proactive agent]] and
close cousin of [[claude-code-channels|Claude Code channels]] (incl. [[claude-code-imessage]]).

Related: [[ai-executive-assistant]], [[agent-memory]].
