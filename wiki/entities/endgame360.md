# Endgame360

A **digital-media holding company** (Minneapolis) founded and chaired by **[[Damien Hoffman]]**. Built
from **The Cheat Sheet** (2008) into a **portfolio of high-traffic content brands** — **Showbiz Cheat
Sheet, Sportscasting, and MotorBiscuit** — that reached **Top-50 most-trafficked** on the internet, made
the **Inc. 5000 multiple times**, and was an **Inc. 2020 Best Workplace**. Damien ran it as Founder & CEO
(2008–2018), then moved to Founder & Board.

## Why it's in the vault
It's **[[Damien Hoffman]]'s** company, and its structure is the reason he's such a sharp mentor for Cole:
**one central engine (ad/content ops + brand) running many properties under a holding company** — the
same shape as [[uship|UShip]]'s 2027 plan (one OS, [[uship-os]], running many campuses under a holdco).
That holdco/M&A lens is what Cole came to the **2026-10-08 mentor call** for ([[damien-hoffman-call-prep]]).
