## Cole's project — *Editing Depth & the Illusion of Explanatory Depth*
**Question:** when you *edit* a knowledge base (vs. read or write it), what happens to your
metacognition — how much you think you **know** and how much you think you **contributed**?
- **Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IoED):** people overrate their understanding until they try to
  explain it in detail (Rozenblit & Keil, 2002). Generating an explanation *breaks* the illusion;
  editing's effect was unclear (Park & Marsh, in prep: reading *inflated* judgments, editing showed
  *no net effect*). Same principle as the learning tactic in [[knowledge-types]] /
  [[learning-by-connection]] — "explain from scratch, never just re-read," because the brain
  mistakes familiarity for understanding (see [[neuroscience-of-behavior]]).
- **Cole's hypothesis:** the null editing effect hides two opposing processes → manipulate **edit
  depth**. **Shallow edits** (fix typos/grammar) stay at the surface "gist" and keep confidence
  *inflated*; **Deep edits** (retrieve + add 3 mechanical details) force retrieval and *calibrate*
  (break the illusion), like a full explanation.
- Also measures **credit attribution** (0–100% "how much of this is mine?") — testing whether
  shallow editors *overclaim* by misattributing the text's fluency to their own authorship.
- **Design:** simulated Wikipedia interface (Qualtrics/SONA), bare-bones stimuli (leap years,
  glass, zippers, moon phases, time zones), 3 conditions (Explain / Shallow / Deep), T1/T2/T3
  ratings. Target **N=250** (~90 collected as of Apr 2026). IRB-approved.
- **Status:** end-of-Spring-2026 write-up done (Apr 29 2026); **continues Fall 2026**.

## Why this connects to *this vault* (worth internalizing)
Cole's research is about the **metacognition of editing a shared knowledge base** — and this
vault is exactly that: an AI-edited knowledge base.
- **Fisher et al. (2015)** — *"the internet inflates estimates of internal knowledge"* — and
  **Eliseev & Marsh (2023)** — *"misattributing the power of the internet to one's own intellect"* —
  are the empirical flip side of the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] / [[second-brain-system]] premise: an
  external, editable knowledge store changes what you *feel* you know.
- His **shallow vs. deep edit** distinction mirrors **[[vault-autoresearch]]**: MODE A (shallow —
  fix defects) vs. MODE B (deep — add new semantic content). The vault literally runs both.
- This is a real instance of Cole's **science ↔ building** through-line (see [[cole|profile]]):
  he *studies* the cognition of knowledge-editing and *builds* a knowledge-editing agent.
