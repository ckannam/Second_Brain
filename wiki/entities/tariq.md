# Tariq

Engineer on the [[claude-code]] team at [[anthropic]]. Author of the blog post **"The
Unreasonable Effectiveness of HTML files"** (given as a talk in San Francisco), arguing that
AI-native development should move from markdown specs to **HTML** for planning, design
review, and verification — see [[html-over-markdown-specs]].

[[how-we-claude-code]] (by Arno) is built on Tariq's talk and repo, including his
verification-native artifact pattern (Storybook fixtures + Playwright MCP, recorded
verification runs). Related: [[fiona-fung]], [[andrej-karpathy]].
