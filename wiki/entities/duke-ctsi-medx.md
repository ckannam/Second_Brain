# Duke CTSI + MEDx — the medical translational engine

Duke's **health/bio translational pillar** of the [[duke-innovation-ecosystem]] — the analog to
[[jhtv|JHTV]]'s [[translational-funding]], but housed in the **medical/research enterprise** rather
than the ventures office. This is Duke's main [[valley-of-death]] bridge for life-science tech.

- **CTSI (Clinical & Translational Science Institute)** — academic hub accelerating **discovery →
  preclinical → clinical trials → implementation → commercialization**. **100+ faculty, ~95 staff**;
  runs Duke's NIH **Clinical and Translational Science Award (CTSA)**; offers translational
  accelerator funding. **[[duke-otc|OTC]]** is a listed partner.
- **MEDx** — since **2015**, catalyzes **medicine × engineering** collaborations and feeds a pipeline
  of early-stage research into CTSI for follow-on translational funding + tech translation.

Most relevant to Cole's **health/bio/AI** lane ([[five-year-plan]]). Related:
[[duke-innovation-ecosystem]] · [[duke-otc]] · [[jhtv]].

Sources: [Duke CTSI](https://ctsi.duke.edu/about-ctsi) · [Duke MEDx](https://medx.duke.edu/research).
