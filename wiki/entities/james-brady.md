# James Brady

Engineer at [[elicit|Elicit]]. In [[trustworthy-agentic-workflows-dsl]] he argues that
**trust depends on mechanism, not just output** ([[mechanism-over-output]]) and presents
Elicit's [[agentic-dsl|AshPL]] design — a Turing-incomplete DSL that keeps the research
agent's process legible, faithful, and iterable.
