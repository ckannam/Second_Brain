# JHU Data Science and AI Institute (DSAI)

Johns Hopkins' university-wide **Data Science and AI Institute** (ai.jhu.edu) — a top-down bet
to make DS/AI a cross-cutting capability across every school, and (the part that matters for
[[jhtv|JHTV]]) a **new pipeline of DS/AI research to commercialize**. This is the "DSAI center"
Cole discusses with [[Kelli Luginbuhl]] as the JHU-side counterpart to Duke's ecosystem debate.

## Scale of the bet (what makes it concrete)
- **80 new tenure-track faculty** across Computer Science, Electrical & Computer Engineering, and
  Applied Mathematics & Statistics — the **largest cluster hire in Hopkins history** — plus **30
  new Bloomberg Distinguished Professors** with cross-disciplinary expertise.
- A **custom-built, state-of-the-art facility** on the Homewood campus with major investment in
  compute/infrastructure.
- **22 new faculty** announced Aug 2025 as the hiring ramped.
- Interim co-directors: **Rama Chellappa** and **KT Ramesh** (senior adviser to the president for
  AI), while an international search ran for a permanent director.

## Why it connects to Cole's world — the commercialization on-ramp
The Institute is deliberately **translational, not just academic**. Two links into [[jhtv|JHTV]]:
- **Demonstration Projects program** — funds *translational* DS/AI (not fundamental research):
  **$100k/year for two years, extendable to five** after a year-two review. Open to faculty,
  clinicians, and APL staff who **already hold external funding**. Goal: prove the gains from
  deploying DS/AI inside an already-funded program → attract **more external funding,
  philanthropy, and partnerships.** (Renewed for 2025.)
- **JHTV expanded its [[translational-funding|Translational Funding]] program with a new DS/AI
  track** — a dedicated non-dilutive lane that feeds Institute research into JHTV's
  commercialization escalator (TF → MII/SBIR → seed → Series A). *(Source Cole flagged:
  ventures.jhu.edu news, "Johns Hopkins expands translational funding program with new support
  for data science and AI innovation.")*

## The takeaway (Cole's framing)
JHU is building the **top-of-funnel** (Institute + faculty + compute) *and* the
**commercialization plumbing** ([[jhtv]] + a DS/AI translational track) at the same time — a
centralized, cross-disciplinary engine. The open question for the Duke comparison: does Duke's
newer DS/AI push have the equivalent **capital + translation scaffolding** behind it, or mostly
the research side? See [[Kelli Luginbuhl]] · [[capital-strategy]] · [[jhtv]].
