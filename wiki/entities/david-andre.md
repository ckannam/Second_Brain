# David Andre

YouTube creator (channel "David Andre") who made **[[autoresearch-tutorial-david-andre]]** — a beginner-focused explainer + live build of [[andrej-karpathy]]'s [[autoresearch]]. Runs an AI-startup accelerator (offers "idea validation calls").

His framing gave the vault its cleanest statement of autoresearch's **three success conditions** (clear scalar metric, automated eval, exactly one editable file) and its **failure modes** (subjective "better," slow loops). Emphasizes that as agent execution becomes free, the scarce skill becomes *choosing the metric* — see [[eval-driven-model-selection]].

One of the creators covering the autoresearch wave alongside [[nick-saraev]] and [[greg-isenberg]].
