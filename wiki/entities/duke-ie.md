# Duke I&E — Innovation & Entrepreneurship Initiative

Duke's **university-wide education, culture & space** pillar of the [[duke-innovation-ecosystem]] —
the entrepreneurial front end that feeds talent and ideas into the pipeline. **Where
[[Kelli Luginbuhl]] & [[Doug Speight]] teach** ([[business-of-science-syllabus-spring2026|The
Business of Science]]) and the home of **Cole's I&E minor**.

## Spaces & programs
- **The Bullpen** — 13,000 sq ft, downtown Durham; opened **Sept 2015**; the I&E hub + workspace for
  **25+ student/alumni startups**.
- **The Foundry** — 7,600 sq ft maker/hardware/robotics project space for all Duke students.
- **Innovation Co-Lab** — creativity incubator (3D printing, woodshop, emerging-tech mentorship).
- **Programs:** I&E+ summer program, **SEAD** (social/health entrepreneurship, w/ Fuqua + Med School
  + Global Health), Melissa & Doug Entrepreneurs, the **Duke Startup Challenge**, the I&E certificate.

Complements [[duke-otc|OTC]] (IP/ventures), [[duke-capital-partners|DCP]] (capital), and
[[duke-ctsi-medx|CTSI/MEDx]] (medical translation). Related: [[Duke]] · [[Kelli Luginbuhl]] ·
[[Doug Speight]].

Sources: [Duke I&E](https://entrepreneurship.duke.edu/) · [Bullpen](https://impact.entrepreneurship.duke.edu/the-bullpen/).
