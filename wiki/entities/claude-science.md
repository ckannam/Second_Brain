# Claude Science

A research **AI workbench** from [[anthropic]] (public beta) for scientists. It runs
analyses, searches databases, and traces every step from data wrangling to validation —
and every artifact ships with the **exact code, environment, and conversation** that
produced it, so it's **reproducible months later by anyone on your team**.

Runs on your laptop, cluster, or on-demand GPUs. Pre-configured for **genomics, single-cell,
proteomics, structural biology, and cheminformatics**, and connects to **60+ scientific
databases** from day one. Available on Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise (macOS + Linux).
Sources: [[claude-science-beta]], [[the-briefing-ai-for-science]]. Docs:
anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench.

Embodies [[ai-for-science]] — collapsing the analysis "toil" so more of the cycle is actual
science. Related: [[elicit]] (research assistant), [[rag]].
