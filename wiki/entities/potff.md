# POTFF — Preservation of the Force & Family

**POTFF** (Preservation of the Force & Family) is the **USSOCOM human performance program** —
the closest thing in DoD to ATP's actual model. It delivers integrated, evidence-based
human-performance services to Special Operations Forces (SOF) and their families through
contracted professionals embedded across SOCOM components.

**Why it matters for ATP:** POTFF's physical domain providers (sports medicine, strength &
conditioning, nutrition, athletic training) are ATP's exact customer profile. The program is a
prime vehicle for ATP to reach a paying SOF customer base — not through a direct government
contract, but via **teaming with the POTFF prime contractor** or a **SOCOM capability
submission** through Engage SOF. See [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]] §5 for the specific moves.

---

## Program structure

POTFF has **four primary lines of effort:**

| Domain | Description |
|---|---|
| **Human Performance** | Sports medicine, strength & conditioning, athletic training, physical therapy, return-to-duty programs |
| **Behavioral Health** | Embedded clinical support, occupational stress management |
| **Spiritual Enhancement** | Chaplain-led resilience, moral injury, meaning-making |
| **Social/Family Programs** | Family support services, resilience programs for military families |

**Core tenets:** performance-focused, evidence-based, centralized oversight with decentralized
execution at the component level (AFSOC, ARSOF, MARSOC, NSW, NSOF, TSOC).

## Contracting vehicle

**Current prime:** **KBRwyle** — contract H9240019D0001, awarded October 2018.
- Original ceiling: $500M; raised ~91% to ~$955M as of 2025 (ceiling ~79% spent).
- Scope: integrated strength & conditioning programs, rehabilitation techniques, athletic
  trainers, S&C coaches, physical therapists, cognitive performance, operational psychology.
- Provides personal services across SOCOM components worldwide.

**POTFF III (follow-on):** USSOCOM posted a hybrid Industry Day (SAM.gov, response date
June 2026) for the POTFF III recompete. No award announced as of this writing (Oct 2026).
The prior estimate of a ~$1.5B POTFF III contract (~Dec 2026 award) is **unverified** —
see [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]] §6 Caveats.

## ATP's play (three paths)

1. **Team with the winning POTFF III prime.** The winning contractor will need
   technology vendors; ATP's markerless motion capture + individualized baseline deviations
   maps onto the S&C and return-to-duty stack. Build the relationship at the **SOFWERX OSBP
   networking event** (Nov 5, Tampa) — that's where POTFF primes meet emerging tech.

2. **Engage SOF / Vulcan capability submission.** Open, continuous; no funding but provides
   SOF data/users via a CRADA (discovery@socom.mil). Validates the product for the POTFF
   ecosystem without a prime-tier contract.

3. **SOCOM standing BAA (HT9425-23-S-SOC1).** Open through July 2028. "Human performance
   optimization" is explicitly listed. Rolling pre-proposal to Grants.gov/eBRAP. Highest-fit
   research funding vehicle independent of the POTFF contracting cycle.
