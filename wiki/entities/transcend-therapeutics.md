# Transcend Therapeutics

**Clinical-stage biotech** building a **rapid-acting, non-hallucinogenic treatment for PTSD.**
Founded **2021**; **acquired by Otsuka** for **$700M upfront + up to $525M milestones (closed June
2026)**. Based in NYC. Where Cole's contact **[[Will Lipman]]** works (Associate, Clinical Operations).

## What they do
- **Lead drug — TSND-201 (methylone).** Methylone is a **chemical analog of MDMA**. Transcend's thesis:
  keep MDMA's therapeutic benefit for PTSD but **remove the psychedelic "trip."** It acts on the
  **monoamine transporters** (raising serotonin, dopamine, norepinephrine) **without engaging the
  5-HT2A receptor** that causes hallucinations.
- Framed as a **"neuroplastogen"** — a compound that promotes **neuroplasticity**, reopening the brain's
  ability to rewire around traumatic memories so recovery can take hold. (Ties to this vault's
  [[neuroscience-of-behavior]] cluster.)
- Pipeline beyond PTSD: also being explored in **major depressive disorder (MDD)** and other CNS
  indications — a platform bet.

## The data & regulatory milestones
- **IMPACT-1 (Phase 2):** 65 severe-PTSD patients; primary endpoint met — **placebo-adjusted CAPS-5
  improvement of −9.64 at Day 64 (p = 0.011)**; significant improvement by **Day 10**, durable to Day 64.
- **Published in JAMA Psychiatry (Feb 2026)** — top-tier journal, a real de-risking milestone.
- **FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation (July 2025).** **Phase 3 recruiting** in the U.S.
- Contrast: **Lykos Therapeutics' MDMA-assisted therapy was FDA-rejected in 2024** — the hallucinogenic
  experience made trials hard to blind and raised safety concerns. Transcend's non-hallucinogenic design
  is a direct answer to that failure.
