# Flourish (neuro-AI startup)

A New York **neuro-AI company** building **Cortex AI**, "the first synthetic intelligence system
designed to match the computational capacity, learning efficiency, and power budget of the human
brain." The bet: current LLMs are powerful but **energy-greedy and static**, so Flourish runs
**original wet-lab neuroscience** to find the brain's **"core algorithm"** and rebuild AI on it.
Target: a synthetic brain on **~50 watts**, that **learns continuously**. Source:
[[flourish-wired-core-algorithm|WIRED, Jun 2026]].

## Facts
- **Funding:** ~**$500M**, reported **$2.5B** valuation. **Jeff Bezos** anchored (~$100M). Others:
  **Lux Capital, Google Ventures, [[catalio-capital|Catalio]]**.
- **Founded:** pitched Bezos Dec 2025; ~2 dozen neuroscientists + AI researchers by Mar 2026.
- **HQ:** West SoHo, NYC (10-story building with a built-in data center). **Actively hiring.**
- **Science:** neuroscientists + AI researchers side-by-side; wet-lab experiments across nano/micro/
  meso scales; focus on **cortical columns** and **connectomes**. Near-term products: a
  **hippocampus-inspired memory** that learns without huge training data, a continuously-learning
  model headed for **pocket devices** and **silicon** (chip-maker talks).
