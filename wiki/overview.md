## Connections worth noting

All three threads rhyme on **friction**. The LLM Wiki wins because it drives the
**maintenance cost of a knowledge base toward zero** — the same reason [[agentic-workflows]]
beat brittle node graphs (agents self-heal instead of demanding manual fixes) and the same
principle as [[environment-design]] and [[cue-routine-reward-loop]]: durable systems come
from **reducing friction**, not from applying more willpower or manual effort. The
[[ai-second-brain-levels|"lowest level that solves your pain"]] rule is the knowledge-work
version of the same idea.
