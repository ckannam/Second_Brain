# Elon Musk — The Economist "Insider" interview (2026)

Long-form interview of [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] by [[the-economist|The Economist]]'s editor-in-chief
Zanny Minton Beddoes at the Texas Gigafactory. Wide-ranging: AI timelines, the economics of
abundance, the China AI race, AI self-regulation, his politics, Starlink/Ukraine. A **snapshot** —
his predictions are claims, not facts; treat dates/odds as his views.

## Core AI thesis
- **AI > the sum of human intelligence in ~5 years** (his guess; ~2031), and "far greater" within 10.
  "There won't be anything AI can't do better than humans apart from being human."
- **An "age of amazing abundance."** The economy = **digital intelligence + physical intelligence**;
  AI supplies the digital, **humanoid robots (Optimus) are the "endectors"** that let it "shape atoms."
  Vast robots × vast digital intelligence ⇒ a "quasi-infinite economy."
- **"Money won't matter in 2036"** and **"work is going to be optional"** — if robots/AI provide more
  goods and services than anyone could consume, the point of money erodes. *(This is the macro version
  of Cole's own [[ai-lowering-entrepreneurship-barriers|AI-lowers-the-barriers]] thesis — and speaks
  directly to his "free + useful" / freedom-as-the-goal frame; see his profile 5-year plan.)*
- **Humans likely not in control in 10 years** — the chimp analogy: if AI is as far above us as we are
  above chimps, "it's hard to imagine the chimpanzees would be in charge." AI safety ⇒ make it
  **"maximally truth-seeking and curious"** so it fosters humanity.
- **On his own p(doom):** still ~10–20% "killer robots" risk, but he's shifted from alarm (2023
  slowdown letter) to **"enjoy the ride"** — acceleration is inexorable; even a stop button probably
  shouldn't be pressed given the abundance upside.

## Industry / competitive read (freshness-relevant to this vault)
- **"Anthropic is currently the leader in AI."** Musk frames the lineage: he founded OpenAI as a
  counterweight to Google, **Anthropic spun out of OpenAI**, and his moves "accelerated AI" unintentionally.
  Corroborates this vault's [[anthropic|Anthropic]]-centric picture.
- **Model lineage matches the vault's:** he calls **[[fable-5|Fable]] "clearly the smartest model,"** says
  **[[mythos|Mythos]]** (which Fable is "derived from") was ready ~February, and that Anthropic almost
  certainly already has something better ("Mythos 2 or Fable 6") ready to release. New name: the Chinese
  **Kimi K3** model, "almost as good as Fable."
- **China AI race:** the binding constraint is **electricity**, not chips — "China has more electricity
  than the US, Europe and India *combined*," heading toward ~4× the US. Strong on humanoid robots too.
  Export controls won't stop China from eventually leading; banning US use of Chinese models "doesn't help."
  Power/cooling — not chips — is the current bottleneck (hence **orbital data centers**). AI's water use is
  "negligible."
- **Self-regulation proposal:** the ~5 frontier labs (Dario, Demis, Sam, Musk) should meet every few weeks
  and get **1–2 weeks to privately test a rival's new frontier model via API** before release, to flag harms
  and recommend a pause. Competitors "keep each other honest" better than non-technical regulators. (Built
  on a Demis Hassabis public-private-regulator proposal Musk called "a good starting point.")

## Politics / power (later chapters)
- **"I got too involved in politics"**; claims **"zero people died because of USAID cuts."**
- On **whether one man should have so much power**, Starlink and the **war in Ukraine**, Europe / the far
  right / "civil war in Britain," and **"I'm not a racist."** Closes on what people get most wrong about him.
