## What it covers
- **Logistics:** Lecture Tu/Th 11:45–1:00 (Gross 107); 9 Wednesday discussion sections (10D–18D); 4 TAs +
  a Learning Assistant (Jack Ringel) for the >200-student enrollment. Textbook: **Tro, *Structure & Properties*, 3e**
  + Mastering Chemistry.
- **Grading:** better of {midterm avg, final} = 50%, lower = 35%; discussion + Mastering HW = 15%. **Resurrection
  quizzes** let students regain up to 25% of lost exam points. 80-70-60-50 grade scale.
- **Key dates:** Midterm 1 **Oct 1**, Midterm 2 **Nov 12**, Final **Dec 9 (2–5pm)**; W-withdraw deadline **Nov 6**.
- **AI policy:** Mastering's embedded AI tutor OK for study; using LLMs on assigned HW discouraged; AI answers to
  in-class Learning Catalytics = potential Duke Community Standard violation. (Shapes how Cole uses this vault for the class.)
- **Course arc (6 modules / ~26 lectures):** Nuclear & Atomic (5) → Solubility/Solutions (4) → Acid–Base (6) →
  Coordination (5) → Electrochemistry (4) → Solid State (2). The syllabus also lists detailed per-module *teaching
  objectives* and the assumed 101/110 learning objectives (thermo, quantum, bonding, kinetics, equilibrium, acids/bases,
  entropy/free energy) — useful for spotting prereq gaps.
