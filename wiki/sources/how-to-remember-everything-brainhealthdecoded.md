# How To Remember Everything You Learn (Brain Health Decoded)

Learning-science essay: we've **optimized for consumption, not learning.** Seeds the learning
half of [[neuroscience-of-behavior]]. Citation-heavy (links to education-research papers).

## Three myths it kills
1. **3× speed ≠ 3× knowledge** — learning is consumption (encoding) *plus* digestion
   (consolidation); most people skip digestion → [[memory-consolidation]].
2. **You must master foundations first** — that's how *children* learn; adults integrate via
   prior knowledge (top-down, plausibility-driven) → [[learning-by-connection]].
3. **All info studied the same way** — the brain has multiple memory systems → [[knowledge-types]].

## The system
- **Step 1 — process before you move on** (the step almost everyone skips): attempt the skill,
  explain the concept aloud, make the flashcard *now*. Unprocessed information disappears.
- **Step 2 — classify** what you're looking at (skill / concept / fact), whether learning by
  choice ("let the walls you face tell you what to learn") or by curriculum.
- **Step 3 — match strategy to type** (see [[knowledge-types]] for the full table): skills →
  practice, try-before-study, short daily reps, stop before sloppy; concepts → ask "why," explain
  aloud, self-built analogies, explain-from-scratch on revisit; facts → atomic spaced-repetition
  cards only.
