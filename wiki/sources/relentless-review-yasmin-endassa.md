# Source: Relentless testimonial — Yasmin Endassa (offer while running her business)

Customer testimonial for **[[relentless]]** (marketing — survivorship bias applies).

**Yasmin Endassa**, a senior analytics executive ("I've delivered insights to **Mark Zuckerberg**"),
landed an offer **while simultaneously scaling her own business** — the search ran "in the background."

What she valued most:
- **Freedom / time** — she'd once spent "literally an entire day to apply to about 20 jobs." Knowing an
  expert team was handling the due diligence (finding recruiters/hiring managers) gave her the freedom to
  operate her business at the same time instead of putting it on hold. Her one-line description: "I just
  wake up in the morning and look at my calendar."
- **Pitch coaching (with [[relentless|Kareem]])** — how to pitch **succinctly**; she was struck that an
  effective outreach email can be **two sentences**, and gained the **confidence** to state her value
  plainly.
- **Role-mapping exercise** — mapping the roles she wanted to the key skills her resume needed to reflect
  (resume was already "decent," so this, not the rewrite, was the big lever).

Her advice on who it's for: **everyone from analyst to CEO**, but it makes *even more* sense **when you
already have a role** — less desperation, more clarity ("always better to look for a job when you have
one"). She plans to use Relentless for **every future search**. "Of all the money I spent, the expense I
have the least regret about."
