# Source — CHEM 210 Unit 2: Solutions & Solubility (lecture slides)

Unit-2 lecture deck for [[chem-210]] (Prof. Cox), 46 slides —
`raw/Processed/CHEM 210 Unit 2 - Solutions and Solubility (lecture slides).pdf`. Despite the "Salts and
Solubility" filename, the deck bundles **four lecture blocks** that together make up the Solutions & Solubility
unit (the ~4-lecture "Solutions & Solubility" arc in the [[chem-210-syllabus-fall-2026|syllabus]]):

1. **Intermolecular Forces & Phase Changes** [p1–15] — bonding triangle, intra- vs inter-molecular, the 4 IMFs
   (dispersion, dipole–dipole, H-bonding, ion–dipole), IMF↔phase, phase diagrams, heating curve (q=mcΔT + ΔH_fus/vap),
   vapor pressure & dynamic equilibrium, Clausius–Clapeyron / pressure–boiling point. LOs on slide 15.
2. **Salts, Lattice Energy & Dissolution Thermodynamics** [p16–27] — solute/solvent/solution, solution interactions,
   lattice energy + crystal structures (rock salt/rutile/fluorite), Born–Haber cycle, NaCl enthalpy of dissolution,
   Gibbs free energy (ΔG=ΔH−TΔS=−RT ln K), the enthalpy/entropy temperature quadrants, ΔS/ΔG for NaCl dissolution,
   concentration units (molarity, ppm, molality). LOs on slide 27.
3. **Colligative Properties** [p28–37] — colligative concept, Raoult's law, Dalton's law of partial pressures (CFC
   mixture example), boiling-point elevation & freezing-point depression (ΔT = i·K·m; K_f/K_b table; van't Hoff factor i),
   osmotic pressure (Π = iMRT) and its applications.
4. **Solubility Equilibrium** [p38–46] — K_sp, Q vs K and direction (ΔG = ΔG° + RT ln Q), equilibrium-constant rules
   (reverse/coefficients/multistep), K_sp calculations, molar solubility (Pb(IO₃)₂), the common-ion effect (TlIO₃ in
   KIO₃), and precipitation (Q vs K_sp; PbCl₂).
