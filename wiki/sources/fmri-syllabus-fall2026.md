# Source: fMRI / Functional Neuroimaging — Syllabus (Fall 2026)

The syllabus for Duke's **Functional Neuroimaging** course. Full operational details live on the
course hub [[fmri]]; this page records what the syllabus itself says.

## Facts
- **Course:** NEUROSCI 382 / PSY 303 · **Fall 2026** · **Tuesdays 3:05–5:45 PM** (two consecutive
  periods with a ~10-min break) · **Perkins LINK 088 (Classroom 4)**.
- **Instructor:** **[[crm/Tobias Overath|Prof. Tobias Overath]]** (LSRC B248A; OH Tue 8:30–9:30 AM;
  t.overath@duke.edu). **TA:** **[[crm/Hector Sanchez Melendez|Hector Sanchez Melendez]]** (M057 DIBS
  Cube; OH Thu 2–3 PM).
- **Textbook:** [[huettel-fmri-textbook|Huettel, Song & McCarthy, *Functional Magnetic Resonance
  Imaging*, 3rd ed. (2014)]].
- **Structure:** each week = a **lecture** (with dense pre-reading) + a **lab** (hands-on data
  analysis in [[spm|SPM]]/MATLAB). First half = MRI/fMRI physics & the [[bold-signal|BOLD]] basis
  → **Midterm I**; second half = design, analysis, clinical applications → **Midterm II**.
- **Group project (30%):** groups of 4 design → program → **scan their own study at [[biac|BIAC]]**
  (two 2-hour sessions, ~2 participants; ~$640/hr) → analyze. Proposal presentation (Week 10),
  results presentation (last class), individual **~3000-word write-up due Tue Dec 8**.

## Grading
**Midterm I 30% · Midterm II 30% · Project 30% · Labs & Quizzes 10%.**

## Policies worth remembering
- **No-tech classroom** — no laptops/phones/tablets during lecture (instructor approval + signed
  agreement required for note-taking exceptions).
- **Attendance mandatory** — submit a STINF for any missed class same-day or lose 2% (up to 10%).
  Missing a day = missing two classes; **>5 missed classes ≈ can't pass.**
- **AI policy (project write-up):** **no generative-AI writing tools** (ChatGPT, AI-Grammarly).
  Basic grammar/spell-check only. ⚠️ *This is the one place in Cole's coursework where AI assistance
  on the deliverable is explicitly prohibited* — the write-up must be his own writing.
- Back up your data; late penalty = 1 letter grade + another per 6 hours late.
