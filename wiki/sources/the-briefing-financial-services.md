# The Briefing: Financial Services

Recap of Anthropic's virtual event with CEOs/CTOs/CIOs of major financial-services firms —
"some of the world's largest banks are now running Claude across their organizations." A
candid conversation on where the industry is and how Anthropic wants to partner.

Themes: **linear rollouts are too slow**; it's not just speed but **speed + security +
scalability**; everyone must change habits and "look at things differently." Historical
framing — like agriculture, electricity, and the internet, this raises living standards, and
capitalist economies recreate jobs — but the pace is uncertain, so **prepare** rather than
fear. Concrete production example in the same vertical: [[signals-that-trade-themselves]]
(Man Group) and [[financial-crime-claude-cowork]]. Companion event:
