# Source — NEUROSCI 206L Week 1: First Class Session (Human Brain)

## Two halves
**1) Orientation (slides 1–14):** faculty/TAs; definition of systems neuroscience; why the course was created (fill gaps in
sensory/motor + human neuroanatomy coverage for the major); scope (sensory before break, motor after); the **TBL weekly rhythm**;
resources (**Lt** + **Sylvius** free for Duke; *Neuroscience* 7e / Oxford Insight); policies (attendance, closed-toed shoes, **AI banned
on assessments**); **team formation = maximize cognitive diversity** (Scott Page, *The Diversity Bonus*); "getting ready for Week 1"
(do the **Getting to Know You survey**, Week 0 module, preview the lab protocol); getting help.

**2) Week 1 anatomy (slides 15–51):** CNS/PNS divisions; neurodevelopment (neural tube → 3 vesicles → adult brain + ventricles);
neurons vs. neuroglia (astrocytes/oligodendrocytes/microglia); gray vs. white matter; cerebral lobes/gyri/sulci + functional themes;
subcortical anatomy; ventricles; meninges (dura/arachnoid/pia); arterial supply (carotid vs. vertebrobasilar, Circle of Willis) +
venous sinuses; anatomical axes & section planes. Figures from *Neuroscience* 6e/5e.
