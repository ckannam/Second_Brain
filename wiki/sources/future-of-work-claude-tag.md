# The future of work with @Claude

[[boris-cherny|Boris Cherny]] (Head of [[claude-code]]) and [[cat-wu|Cat Wu]] (Head of
Product) on **[[claude-tag|Claude Tag / @Claude]]** — a proactive, multiplayer agent — and
the long-horizon + alignment + memory work behind it.

## The shift
"In the past you had to open Claude and ask. **With Claude Tag, Claude jumps in.**" It's
proactive (knows when to jump in), does work that takes **days or weeks**, follows up, and
**remembers for next time** — and it's **multiplayer**: add @Claude to a Slack channel and
the whole team sees and participates.

Two leaps in two years: typeahead (write a line) → whole functions/files/features → **the
whole thing end-to-end** (a feature, an experiment, data analysis). The human's role moved
from one-person-in-the-loop → one person driving ~10 Claudes → **a whole team** collaborating
with Claude that "drives a little more." Enabled by long-horizon + alignment research (stay
on track for days) and a memory system that "finally feels right." Data point: **65% of the
[Claude Code] product team's code is created by Claude.**

Claude Tag is in Slack (beta) — claude.com/tag. Related: [[proactive-agents]],
[[claude-code-channels]], [[ai-executive-assistant]], [[ai-native-engineering-org]],
[[agent-memory]].
