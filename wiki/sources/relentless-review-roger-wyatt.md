# Source: Relentless testimonial — Roger Wyatt (+$60K, Director of Engineering)

Customer testimonial for **[[relentless]]** (marketing — read with survivorship bias in mind).

**Roger Wyatt**, a seasoned tech leader, landed **Director of Engineering at Duck Creek Technologies**
— **$195K base + $20K bonus**, a **$60K increase** over his previous role. Solo, he was grinding out
~1 interview/week; with Relentless he averaged ~**4/week** (best week **8**).

What he credits:
- **Resume rewrite** — he'd already paid for a "better" resume, but it "wasn't the way people are looking
  at it now." Two lessons: **tech people undersell their impact** ("we don't take credit the way
  salespeople do") — so **quantify it** — and **name-drop recognizable brands** (he'd done work for
  **NASA** and **Capital One**).
- **Backchannel outreach** — Relentless emailing people behind the scenes is "how Duck Creek came about"
  (someone passed his note up the chain until he got noticed). He stresses how bad **ghosting** is at the
  senior level.
- **Headspace** — outsourcing freed his mental capacity ("you wake up and all you think about is applying
  to more jobs").

Caveats he names: it only pays off if the **salary/increase is big enough** to justify it (he was after
Director/VP/CTO roles, which are scarcer than senior-IC roles), and geography mattered (he wanted
Richmond, not fully remote).
