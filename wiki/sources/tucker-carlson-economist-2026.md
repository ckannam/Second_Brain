# Tucker Carlson — The Economist "Insider" interview (March 2026)

Combative long-form interview of [[tucker-carlson|Tucker Carlson]] by [[the-economist|The Economist]]'s
editor-in-chief Zanny Minton Beddoes. Framing: Carlson has become **MAGA's most prominent dissident** after
breaking with Trump's White House over the **Iran war** ("Operation Epic Fury"), which he called "disgusting
and evil." A **snapshot of his stated positions**, not endorsements.

## Positions (as argued)
- **Iran war = world-historic mistake.** The real stake is **control of the Persian Gulf / Strait of Hormuz
  (~22% of world energy)**, on which key Asian allies (Japan, Taiwan, South Korea) wholly depend — "who
  controls that" is the only measure of who won. He argues the biggest beneficiary is **Russia** (a country
  the West "never mentions"), not necessarily China.
- **Campaign-finance / foreign-lobby critique.** Says the war's advocates "funded the last election" and that
  a **foreign lobby** is the biggest force in US politics — the reason the Senate "goes along with stuff like
  this."
- **Gaza:** the initial post-Oct-7 response was "reasonable," but the current state is **"a disaster"** — for
  the Palestinians, for Israel's future, and a moral **"horror" (70,000 dead)**. Argues Israel should be
  *constrained for its own benefit* before it escalates to a catastrophic (even nuclear) outcome.
- **"Zionist"?** Refuses the label; demands the term be defined; insists on **universal human rights, not
  ethnic rights** — "if standards aren't universally applicable, they're preferences."
- **Ukraine:** won't call it **"a sovereign country"**; blames the **US government** (State/CIA/"permanent
  agencies," Biden administration) for provoking Russia by pushing Ukraine toward **NATO**; calls the
  **SWIFT expulsion + sanctions** the "most reckless and destructive" step. Not pro-invasion, but says US
  support was strategically self-harming. Notes essentially all current aid is European-funded.
- **Taiwan:** "**The US is not going to defend and cannot defend Taiwan**" — "Taiwan is already kind of not a
  real thing anymore." Great powers control their regions (Monroe-Doctrine analogy); the US must accept
  **power-sharing with China** (now the world's largest economy).
- **Europe:** *is* America's deepest ally and should be treated like one — he opposes the elite US "hatred of
  Europe" and the Greenland threats, even while mocking European leaders (Macron, Merz, Starmer) as "buffoons."
