# The Briefing: AI for Science

Recap of Anthropic's virtual event where leading researchers and pharma companies shared what
they've built on Claude — and the launch venue for **[[claude-science|Claude Science]]**.

Framing: two years ago Anthropic's CEO argued AI could compress **50–100 years** of progress
in biology/medicine into **5–10** ([[ai-for-science]]); the briefing is the case that it has
started. Key line: an experiment that takes 3 days to run but 3+ weeks to analyze — "those
weeks aren't science, they're the **toil** you endure to get to the science," and that toil
is collapsing. Speakers stress AI as a *starting point* ("no human would have thought of
that") that still needs human experimental science, creativity, and discipline. Ambition
voiced: "**10 years of progress every year**," and teams of "bilingual" people fluent in both
a scientific domain and AI.
