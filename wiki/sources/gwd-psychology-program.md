# Source — Duke P&N: Graduation with Distinction (GwD)

Duke Psychology & Neuroscience department page laying out the **Graduation with Distinction**
program — eligibility, requirements, and the year-long timeline. Separate processes for
**Psychology** vs **Neuroscience** majors. Relevant to Cole via his [[marsh-memory-lab|Marsh Lab]]
research ([[psy-394-marsh-lab]]); the durable pathway/tracker lives at [[gwd-thesis-pathway]].

## Psychology track (the one that applies to Cole)
**Eligibility:** PSY major (AB/BS/…) or related IDM. Overall Trinity GPA ≥ 3.0; **Psych GPA ≥ 3.5
by fall of senior year** (excludes Independent Study & Distinction Thesis Workshop grades; not
rounded). You may register without meeting GPA but won't *receive* Distinction unless met at graduation.

**Requirements:**
- **Two semesters of Research Independent Study** on the project with the **same** faculty member;
  the **second semester must be PSY 495**. (Timeline names PSY 493 as the fall RIS.)
- **IRB approval before enrolling in PSY 496** (Distinction Thesis Workshop, spring senior year).
- Defend thesis before a **3–6 person committee**.
- Present at the **Psychology GwD Poster Session**.
- Submit electronic thesis to the Psych DUS Office by the last day of spring exams.
- **Faculty chair of the committee must be a *primary* P&N faculty member — no exceptions.** The RIS
  supervisor serves as chair unless they aren't primary P&N. ([[Elizabeth Marsh]] qualifies.)

**Timeline (key dates):**
- End of spring **junior** year — pick faculty supervisor, start RIS proposal.
- **Fall senior year** — enroll **PSY 493**, get **IRB approval**, identify **2 more committee members**.
- **GwD application form due — November 9, 2026** (end of regular registration).
- **Spring** — enroll **PSY 496** Distinction Thesis Workshop.
- **January** — set defense date, book room via 25Live (defenses late March → mid-April).
- **Defend by April 13** to be eligible for the **Karl E. Zener Award**.
- Day before last day of class — **poster session**.
- Final thesis due last day of exams, **or April 24, 2027** to be Zener-eligible.
- Optional: deposit final paper in **DukeSpace**.

**Funding / support:** Office of Undergraduate Research Support (URS), **Lafitte Undergraduate
Research Awards**; a designated Psychology librarian and Duke Libraries honors resources.
Contact: `psychologyDUS@duke.edu`; GwD Handbook (Box) + flyer for the most current forms/deadlines.

## Neuroscience track (for reference — not Cole's path)
Similar shape: GPA ≥ 3.5 in neuro courses, ~two semesters of NEUROSCI 493/494 with one mentor,
spring **NEUROSCI 499** *Current Research in Neuroscience*, oral defense + written thesis + April
poster session (dates on the page are from a 2023 cycle). Committee of ~3 incl. a neuro A&S faculty
member; a postdoc/advanced grad student may serve. Contact was tyler.lee@duke.edu.
