# Source: ATP Non-Dilutive Funding Plan (Nov 1, 2026)

A dated internal memo (provided by Cole, Nov 1 2026) that audits an ATP funding shortlist
Jaylen assembled, **corrects** several items, and lays out a Nov 2026–Jun 2027 timeline. Its
center of gravity is **North Carolina state + Duke non-dilutive money** — a lane the vault had
not yet mapped (the existing [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]] is federal/DoD only). Full
synthesis lives in the companion concept page [[atp-nc-nondilutive-funding]]; this page records
the source and the **reconciliation against ATP's own Drive docs** that the memo author could not
do.

> **Source posture.** The memo cites third-party consultants (bwcoconsulting), state agencies
> (NC Commerce, NC IDEA), and program pages, and **explicitly flags its own unverified items**
> (the Nov 4 xTech event; the NV068 Phase I dollar figure; unpublished Spring 2027 NC IDEA dates;
> ATP's founder full-time status, revenue, and Secretary-of-State registration date). It also says
> it did **not** re-check the federal tracker items (CDMRP, 711th HPW BAA, SOCOM) against official
> sources — those remain owned by [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]].

## The memo's bottom line

- **First move: file the One NC Incentive claim for the Navy SBIR proposal now** (target by Nov 15).
  It is the only state money ATP can reach without *winning* anything — Durham is Tier 3 (50% of
  documented eligible costs, capped at $12K), funded first-come from a ~$210K pool. Two
  pre-checks: confirm ATP was registered with the NC Secretary of State in time, and that the
  claim clears ~$1–2K to be worth filing.
- **NC IDEA: apply for MICRO ($10K), not SEED ($50K)** — unless a founder is genuinely full-time
  (NC IDEA's bar: 40+ hrs/wk on the startup, ≤15 hrs/wk of school). Full-time Duke students don't
  meet that on their face.
- **Three shortlist corrections:** NCBiotech deadlines on Jaylen's list are wrong (and its
  loan-plus-warrant is a poor fit — skip for now); SEED is likely blocked without a full-time
  founder; the **Nov 4 "xTech|Search 10 pitch" matches nothing on the Army's published schedule**.
- **NSF SBIR:** next full-proposal deadline after Nov 4 is **March 4, 2027** (memo corrects
  Jaylen's "Mar 3"); Phase I up to $305K; requires a Project Pitch invite first (1–2 mo response).

## ✅ Reconciliation against ATP's Drive (what the memo author couldn't verify)

Done by Cole/Claude on ingest, Nov 1 2026, against the shared ATP Google Drive:

1. **NV068 Phase I dollar figure — RESOLVED.** The memo left "$240K vs $315K" open. ATP's
   **`ATP_Navy_SBIR_Vol3_Cost_Volume.xlsx`** states **Ceiling $315,000 (Base + Option)** for topic
   **DON26BZ05-NV068**, and ATP's proposed total is **$314,168** (Base ≈ $196,997 / Option ≈
   $117,170). So the memo's **$315K is correct**; the $140K+$100K Release-1 figure does **not**
   apply to ATP's Release-5 submission. → Use **$315K** as the base for any One NC Matching math.
2. **The Nov 4 xTech event is absent from ATP's own tracker too.** ATP's **Federal / DoD
   Opportunity Tracker** (Drive) lists only **xTech|Search 10 (white paper due Oct 19)** and
   **xTech|Kinetic Reach (~Dec 2026)** — **no Nov 4 pitch**. This corroborates the memo: treat
   Wednesday as an informal/program-office engagement until ATP holds the invitation email.
3. **NV068 is the proposal ATP actually submitted** — not a "watch-for-reposts" topic. ATP's
   **Navy SBIR intake questionnaire** is headed *"Topic DON26BZ05-NV068 — Intelligent Tools for
   Naval Aircrew Performance and Readiness."* Vol 2 was finalized **Sep 23, 2026**, matching the
   memo's Aug 26–Sep 23 receipt window. (This corrects the [[atp-federal-funding-landscape]]
   framing that had NV068 as merely a closed topic to watch.)
4. **SEED full-time question — a real nuance.** The intake questionnaire certifies **Jaylen
   Coleman has "primary employment with ATP"** (the SBIR PI primary-employment rule). That's not
   identical to NC IDEA's 40+/≤15 test, but it means the memo's blanket "full-time Duke students
   don't qualify" **may be too pessimistic for Jaylen specifically** — verify his actual hours /
   school load before defaulting to MICRO.
5. **Still open (memo was right to flag):** ATP's **NC Secretary-of-State registration date**,
   **revenue** (<$250K?), and **ownership** (did ATP take DRF/other VC money — the >50% US-citizen/PR
   control test for xTech, and the <$250K prior-investment line for NC IDEA). The Drive's
   **`ATP_Investment_Summary`** is a product overview with no cap table; these need Jaylen.

## Key figures captured (for the concept page)

- One NC Incentive: 50% Tier 3, cap **$12K**, rolling to **Jun 30, 2027**, ~**$210K** pool, one
  award/company/solicitation period; needs SOS registration before federal submission (or ≥90 days
  before the claim), a written COI policy, ≥51% work in NC, no overdue taxes.
- One NC Matching: 50% up to **$75K**, ~**$1.92M** pool, FY2027 solicitation to be released **Dec
  14, 2026**; needs an executed Phase I award. **Only a Phase I award triggers the match** — xTech's
  $5K/$20K prizes do not.
- NC IDEA MICRO $10K (15 awards/cycle, ~4% accept Spring 2026); SEED $50K (6 awards, ~3%); one of
  MICRO or SEED per cycle; "not a research grant" → pitch milestones.
- NSF SBIR/STTR: Phase I ≤$305K (NSF 26-510), Phase II ≤$1.25M; pitch windows + Mar 4, 2027 full
  proposal; Jul 27 / Nov 4 / Mar 4 deadlines.
- Programs the memo adds to pursue: **NIH SBIR** (PA-27-100; due Jan 5 & Apr 5, 2027), **NC-COMP**
  (RTI-led teaming consortium — Duke is a founding member), **NC DEFTECH** (NCMBC free DoD SBIR/OTA
  help), **Duke Startup Showcase / I&E Follow-on ($5K) / Seed ($25K)**, **SBTDC** counseling.
- xTech published schedule (matches [[xtech-search]]): white papers Oct 19 → ≤50 semifinalists
  ($5K) announced **Jan 5, 2027** → virtual pitches **Jan 18–29** → finals **Mar 2–4** (eMerge
  Americas; $200K/$100K/$50K) → finalists only may file Army Phase I (≤$300K) **Mar 4–Apr 30, 2027**.
