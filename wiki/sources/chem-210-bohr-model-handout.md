# Source — CHEM 210 Bohr Model handout (Prof. Cox, May 2025)

Two-page derivation handout for [[chem-210]] Unit 1. Raw PDF in `raw/Processed/CHEM 210 Bohr Model handout (Cox).pdf`.
Walks the full Bohr-model derivation from force balance to the radius and energy expressions. Per the Unit 1 overview,
**the derivations themselves are not tested** — but the resulting relationships and the two force expressions are.
Synthesized into [[chem-210-quantum-atomic-structure]].

## The derivation (traceable steps)
1. **Forces:** centripetal (Coulomb, toward nucleus) `F = Ze²/kr²` balances centrifugal (outward) `F = mv²/r`.
   `e = 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C`; k = Coulomb constant.
2. **Quantization:** Bohr restricts angular momentum, `mvr = nh/2π`.
3. Solve for v, substitute back → **radius** `r = h²kn²/4π²me²Z`; constants collapse to the **Bohr radius a₀ = 52.9 pm**,
   giving `r = a₀·n²/Z`.
4. **Energy:** `E_total = ½mv² + PE` with `PE = −Ze²/kr`. Using the force relation, KE = −½PE, so `E_total = −½·Ze²/kr`.
   Substituting r gives `E_total = −2.18×10⁻¹⁸ J · Z²/n²`.

## Why it matters
Anchors the qualitative relationships CHEM 210 tests: deeper n → smaller r, higher v, more-negative E; higher Z →
tighter, lower-energy orbits; and the KE/PE (virial) link. Bridges into wave–particle duality and the Schrödinger picture.
