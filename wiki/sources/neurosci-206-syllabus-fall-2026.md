## What it covers
- **Required foundational course** for the Duke Neuroscience major (alongside NEUROSCI 217D & 223; may be taken in any order).
- **Team-Based Learning** format: Mon lecture (optional) → Wed Readiness Assurance (iRA + tRA, graded) → Thu/Fri lab.
- **Grading:** RAs 20%+20%, midterm 20%, lab reports 20%, final 20% → **60% individual / 40% team**. Straight scale (no curve);
  top 10 of ~13 RAs and ~13 lab reports count. Human brain specimens + physiology experiments (nerve conduction, EMG, EOG).
- **Textbook:** *Neuroscience* 7e (OUP), by course faculty; earlier 4th–6th eds adequate. Sylvius atlas + **Lt** software both free for Duke.
- **AI policy:** prohibited on all in-class assessments (RA, midterm, final); allowed on lab reports with disclosure + query copy.
- **Key dates:** Midterm **Oct 14**, Final **Dec 13 (7–10 PM)**; fall break Oct 9–13; Thanksgiving Nov 24–29.
- **15-week schedule:** Wk1 intro+neuroanatomy → Unit 1 Sensation (Wk2–7: principles, visual, auditory, vestibular, somatosensory,
  chemical) → midterm → Unit 2 Movement (Wk9–15: lower/upper motor, basal ganglia, cerebellum, eye movements, visceral). Chapters mapped on [[neurosci-206]].
- **8 learning objectives** centered on localizing CNS structures and describing ascending sensory / descending motor pathways + sensory/motor physiology.
