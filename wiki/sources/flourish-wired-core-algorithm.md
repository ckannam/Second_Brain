# Source: WIRED — Bezos funds Flourish's hunt for the brain's "core algorithm"

Steven Levy's June 2026 launch profile of **[[flourish|Flourish]]**, the neuro-AI startup that
raised **$500M at a reported $2.5B valuation** with **Jeff Bezos** anchoring. Basis for the
[[flourish]] entity page and the [[target-orgs]] outreach angle. Cited throughout those pages.

## The thesis
Frontier LLMs are powerful but **energy-greedy and static**: a person runs on ~20 watts; a single
AI-cluster chip uses 30×+ that, and hyperscalers burn gigawatts. And once trained, LLMs **don't
learn**. Reardon's framing: "There's something fundamentally wrong with saying, 'I need to read
every book ever written 20 times to learn English.' A human baby does it with a couple hundred
thousand utterances." Flourish's bet: put **real neurons under the microscope** (original wet-lab
neuroscience) to find the brain's **"core algorithm,"** and build **Cortex AI** — a synthetic brain
targeting **~50 watts**, continuous learning, and human-like adaptability.

## How they work
- **Neuroscientists and AI researchers side by side.** Wet-lab experiments (electron microscopes,
  multimillion-dollar microscopy) across **nano / micro / meso scales**; algo team builds models
  informed by the biology, and vice versa. Focus: **cortical columns** ("the canonical computational
  unit" of the brain) and **connectomes**.
- **Near-term revenue paths:** a **hippocampus-inspired memory** approach to learn without huge
  training data; a **continuously-learning model** being embodied in **pocket devices**; in talks
  with a major chip maker to put the model **on silicon**. Open to publishing some original research.
- **Timeline/scale:** pitched Bezos **Dec 2025**; ~two dozen top neuroscientists + AI researchers by
  end of March 2026; moved into a **West SoHo NYC** 10-story building with a built-in data center.
  Reardon hopes for the "big solution" in ~5 years; Williams frames it as a 7–10 year bet.

## Money
$500M round, ~$2.5B valuation. **Bezos** put in $50M after reading Williams' two-pager, then nearly
doubled it (~$100M) and said he'd have given more. Other backers: **Lux Capital, Google Ventures,
[[catalio-capital|Catalio]]**, among others.

## People (see CRM + entity page)
- **[[Thomas Reardon]]** — co-founder; neuroscientist + repeat founder. Built Microsoft's Internet
  Explorer; founded CTRL-Labs (neural-interface wristband) → sold to Meta. Columbia classics degree
  then neuroscience doctorate. (Wikipedia notes an earlier **MS in Neurobiology from Duke**; Wired's
  bio omits it and he identifies strongly with Columbia — so the Duke tie is minor.)
- **Rob Williams** — co-founder; ex-Amazon **S-team** exec (ran Alexa software); knew Reardon from
  Microsoft. Pitched Bezos.
- **[[Joshua Vogelstein]]** — co-founder; **Johns Hopkins BME professor**; coauthored a fruit-fly
  connectome paper finding the fly network **10× more efficient than a transformer**. Co-founded the
  JHU **Open Connectome Project**. ← the [[jhtv|Hopkins]] link that matters for Cole.
- **Jacob Vogelstein** — investor/adviser; neuroscientist-turned-VC; **managing partner of
  [[catalio-capital|Catalio Capital]]** (Baltimore/NYC); Joshua's brother; co-Open Connectome. Frames
  the company as "looking for the algorithms underlying intelligence."
- **Advisers/team:** **Greg Wayne** (DeepMind, heads Project Astra; 20% time), **Ben Recht** (UC
  Berkeley, skeptical-but-hopeful), **Vish Sivakumar**, plus neuroscientists **Nathan Danielson**,
  **Sean Bittner**, **Josh Morgan** (several ex-Meta with Reardon).
