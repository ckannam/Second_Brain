# Teaching agents to learn from your team

Short session (clip is the blurb) on an agent that **improves itself daily by treating
instructions as code** — edited, reviewed, and merged like any PR (see
[[instructions-as-code]]). Emphasis on writing **[[claude-code-skills|skills]] that teach
agents how to *think*, not what to do**, and **closing the feedback loop** so team judgment
flows back into the agent automatically.

A lightweight, per-team analogue of [[agent-dreaming]] (self-improvement via curated memory)
and [[governed-skills-framework]] (shared, reviewed skills). Related: [[claude-code-memory]],
[[ai-native-engineering-org]].
