# Introducing Claude Corps (Anthropic announcement, 2026-07-30)

Anthropic's announcement of **[[claude-corps]]**, a national fellowship placing early-career
people at US nonprofits for a paid year of AI-enabled work. Summarized here; the decision-facing
read for Cole lives on the entity page **[[claude-corps]]**.

## What it is
- A **12-month, full-time, in-person fellowship**. Fellows are trained to use Claude well, matched
  to a host nonprofit, and paid to advance that org's mission for a year.
- **1,000 fellows** targeted; **≥400 nonprofits** hosting over the next 12 months. Initial
  **$150M** commitment; ambition to scale far beyond and replicate internationally.
- **Three-org partnership:** **[[anthropic]]** funds/strategy/Claude expertise · **[[codepath]]**
  is the fellows' *employer of record* + leads programming · **Social Finance** leads measurement
  and builds the scaling vehicle.

## The fellow package
- **$85,000 salary + benefits**, a CodePath mentor, Anthropic technical office hours, an expansive
  Claude token budget, and a manager at the host org.
- Intensive upfront Claude training, then **5 hrs/week ongoing training**; rest of time on the host org.

## Eligibility
- Over 18, **under two years of full-time work experience**, US work authorization, comfortable
  with Claude, willing to relocate (relocation support available). **Any educational background.**

## Timeline (the key facts for Cole)
- **Cohort 1** (100 fellows): applications **closed July 17**, starts **October 2026**.
- **Rolling applications** now open for the next two cohorts, starting **January 2027** and
  **August 2027**.
- Host-org applications open for all start dates.

## Named host orgs (NC ones matter for Cole)
Braven (Chicago) · **Code the Dream (Durham, NC)** · Heartland Forward (Bentonville, AR) ·
Montgomery County Food Bank (Conroe, TX) · Team RWB (Floyds Knobs, IN) · Reef Environmental
(Key Largo, FL) · SoundOff (San Antonio, TX) · StriveTogether (Cincinnati, OH) ·
**YMCA of Greater Charlotte (NC)**.
