## Authoritative timeline (supersedes Cole's brief)
- **Fri Aug 21, 12:00pm (noon) ET** — internal campus-review deadline: click **"Ready for Review"** / lock the application in the national portal. **Do not hit submit.** (Cole's brief said "Aug 21" without the noon cutoff.)
- **Thu Sept 10, 12:00pm (noon) ET** — deadline for **recommendation letters + foreign-language evaluations** (uploaded via portal by the writers). *This hard rec-letter deadline was absent from Cole's brief — it's the real constraint on lining up recommenders.*
- **Tue Oct 6, 4:59pm ET** — final national submission.

## Endorsement (a reassuring detail)
Duke's endorsement is **not competitive and not capped** — NCS endorses *all* Duke-affiliated applicants who apply. Working with NCS is optional (no penalty for going it alone). Endorsement = they upload a strengths evaluation to national reviewers. Post-campus-deadline: they review (late Aug), unlock apps so you keep polishing (by Sept 2), do an informational interview (Sept), write endorsement letters (late Sept/early Oct), submit by Oct 6.
