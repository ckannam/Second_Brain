# Conscious vs Subconscious Mind (ixcarus)

Substack deep dive (author *ixcarus*) on how the mind runs — the model layer of
[[neuroscience-of-behavior]]. Seeds [[conscious-vs-subconscious]], [[reticular-activating-system]],
and [[cognitive-biases]].

> **Filename note:** this was clipped as `How To Remember Everything You Learn 1.md` — a
> collision with the unrelated [[how-to-remember-everything-brainhealthdecoded]] piece. Its real
> subject is the conscious/subconscious mind, not memory technique.

## Key takeaways
- **[[conscious-vs-subconscious|Dual-process mind]]** — a tiny conscious mind (~7±2 items, ~40–50
  bits/s) rides a vast subconscious (~11M bits/s). Learning = conscious struggle → subconscious
  automation (the driving example).
- **Emotions are subconscious programs**, often installed in childhood; they fire before
  conscious reasoning — why knowing ≠ feeling.
- **[[reticular-activating-system]]** — the brainstem filter you program with conscious focus;
  confirmation bias on autopilot.
- **[[cognitive-biases]]** — heuristics (confirmation, availability, anchoring, sunk-cost, FAE) as
  speed-over-accuracy features, countered only by conscious awareness.
- **Reprogramming needs repetition + emotion** (not knowledge) → same lever as [[neuroplasticity]];
  integration happens in sleep/dreams → [[memory-consolidation]].

## Notes / reconciliation
Ranges from solid cognitive science to speculation. **Well-supported:** limited working memory
(7±2), dual-process cognition (cf. Kahneman System 1/2), RAS filtering, listed biases, sleep
consolidation. **Treat as framing, not fact:** the "decision 7 seconds before awareness" claim
(overstates Libet/Soon-style findings), "subliminals," "calcified pineal gland," and the
**superconscious/collective-unconscious** section (the author flags it as theory). Freud/Jung
invoked loosely. Same author as [[neuroplasticity-rewiring-brain-ixcarus]].
