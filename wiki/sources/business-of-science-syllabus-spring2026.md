# The Business of Science (I&E 710.01) — Syllabus, Spring 2026

Duke graduate I&E course, **co-taught by [[Doug Speight]] and [[Kelli Luginbuhl]]** (TA Kol
Rollins). Wednesdays 3:05–5:35pm, General Motors Classroom. The class Cole took with them — the
source of the [[technology-commercialization|commercialization frameworks]] he learned, including
the [[valley-of-death]].

## What the course is
Equips students to **transition lab technologies to the commercial marketplace** — the language,
frameworks, and IP-assessment tools, plus the **motivations and incentives of key stakeholders**
(scientists, universities, government, investors, customers). Hands-on: teams use **AI tools to
assess a portfolio of invention disclosures**, pick high-potential technologies, run market
assessments, and build a go-to-market plan → final commercialization plan for one technology.

## Learning objectives
- Principles of tech commercialization + the **stages** of the process.
- The **stakeholder landscape** and the impact of **complex human behavior** on commercialization.
- Use **AI tools** to assess commercial potential of IP within a portfolio.
- Build a framework for **articulating a commercialization strategy.**

## Schedule → key concepts (the useful map)
- **Wk 1** — Commercialization phases; key concepts.
- **Wk 2** — Stakeholder landscape mapping; academic-innovation case studies; **TTOs** & research incentives.
- **Wk 3** — **[[Duke OTC]]** (guest **Robin Rasor**): TTO practices/policies; **SBIR/STTR**; public-private partnerships.
- **Wk 4** — **Investor perspectives & "transactional intelligence"**: VC vs. strategics; the transaction cycle's effect on team dynamics.
- **Wk 5** — VC/angel panel; **misaligned incentives vs. commercialization goals**.
- **Wk 6–7** — **AI in technology assessment**: scouting/evaluating invention disclosures; funnel to high-potential techs; team formation.
- **Wk 8–9** — **Customer discovery & market assessment** (size, trends, competitive landscape); down-select to one tech; thesis & assumptions.
- **Wk 10–11** — **Articulating commercialization & GTM**: the **H5W framework**; **tech/market/execution risk**; the **commercialization brief**.
- **Wk 12** — Career paths in commercialization.
- **Wk 13–14** — Final strategic recommendations + team presentations.

## Grading
30% attendance/participation · 40% team assignments · 30% final presentation & write-up. AI use
**permitted and encouraged** (but not 100%-AI submissions).
