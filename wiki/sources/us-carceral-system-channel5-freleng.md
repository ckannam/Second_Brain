## The substantive reporting (encoded in [[mass-incarceration]])
- **Scale:** the US has the developed world's highest incarceration rate — ~**1.8M** people incarcerated;
  a **majority are non-violent offenders** (she cites ~70–80%; ~40% for non-violent drug-era convictions).
- **Mandatory sentencing & juveniles:** California's **1994 "three strikes"** law and the **"super-predator"**
  era produced **juvenile life-without-parole** under *mandatory* schemes that stripped judges of
  discretion. The Supreme Court only ruled mandatory JLWOP unconstitutional in **2012–2014**, releasing
  people sentenced as children — e.g. **"Suave"** (Philadelphia, 17 at offense; subject of her Pulitzer-
  winning podcast — the 3rd podcast ever to win one).
- **Wrongful convictions ~10% of the prison population** ("hundreds of thousands"); some rest on
  discredited forensics (e.g. **shaken-baby-syndrome** cases where no crime occurred).
- **Re-entry is engineered to fail.** Parole you must *pay for*; **lifetime parole** where one misdemeanor
  (jaywalking, a missed payment) returns you to prison; Louisiana **child-support debt** that blocks an ID
  or job above a ~$1,500 balance → pushed back to the street economy → recidivism.
- **Private-prison economics:** **CoreCivic** and **GEO Group** run private prisons *and* ICE detention on
  a per-diem **bed-rate** model (paid per person held → incentive to lock up and hold), with **labor as the
  biggest cost slice** and near-free **prison labor** (Angola cited as literally a former plantation). ICE
  detention notably **oversees itself** (unlike DOJ facilities).
