**Thesis:** A 7-day plan (proven by student **Christian**, who signed in 5) to land your first AI automation client via warm outreach and a tiny free pilot.

- **Day 1:** set direction + build a "trust map" of warm contacts.
- **Days 2–3:** warm conversations, not sales calls.
- **Days 4–6:** build a **tiny free pilot**.
- **Day 7:** convert pilots into paid work.
- Mindset shift + three psychological hurdles to overcome; then a repeatable loop.
