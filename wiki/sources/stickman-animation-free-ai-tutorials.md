## Shared workflow (the through-line)
**Voice-first, images second** — narration pauses define scene cuts. Chatbot writes script + a
scene-by-scene breakdown (image prompt + animation prompt per beat); generate one visual per
scene/timestamp; keep the **character consistent via a reference image**; assemble to the voice in
a free editor with auto-captions; package with a researched title + a thumbnail made from a
generated frame.

## Tips worth keeping
- **AI stock voices are getting channels demonetized** — creators recommend a **custom/own voice**.
  For a personal educational brand like Neuro, **Cole recording his own voice is the safest and
  most on-brand** ($0). See [[neuro-free-tool-stack]].
- **Character consistency** is the whole game for a mascot — upload the reference (`neuro.png`) and
  let the model hold the design; generate 2 images/scene and discard the mangled one.
- **Packaging > prompts:** copy title/thumbnail patterns from viral outliers in-niche; don't trust
  a raw "make a viral title" prompt.
- These are **snapshots** — free credit limits, model names (Nano Banana 2, VO3), and monetization
  rules change fast; re-verify before relying on any single tool's free tier.
