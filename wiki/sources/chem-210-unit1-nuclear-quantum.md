## Key facts from the slides (traceable anchors)
- Discovery arc: Becquerel/Curie (radioactivity as atomic property) → Thomson (electron) → Rutherford (nucleus) →
  Richards (odd masses) → mass spec (isotopes, e.g. Mg-24/25/26).
- **Mass-defect worked example (slide 9):** Fe-56 (55.921 amu) → binding energy. `1 amu = 1.66×10⁻²⁷ kg`.
- Decay equations given: α (²²⁶Ra→²²²Rn), β⁻ (³H→³He), β⁺ (²¹Na→²¹Ne); fission mass-balance for U-235.
- Decay kinetics medical problems: renal-binding radioisotope (activity→mass), Na-24 fraction remaining.
- **Bohr:** `r = a₀n²/Z` (a₀ = 52.9 pm); `E = −2.18×10⁻¹⁸ J·Z²/n²`. de Broglie `λ = h/mv`.
- **Particle-in-a-box:** `E_n = n²h²/8mL²` → E∝n², E∝1/L², nodes = n−1.
- Orbitals: radial nodes = n−ℓ−1, angular = ℓ, total = n−1; no 1p (ℓ≤n−1); Aufbau exceptions **Cr, Cu**.
- **Slater's rules** for shielding (0.35 / 0.85 / 1.00); Z_eff drives IE, radii, EN.
- Three electronegativity scales: Pauling (bond energies), Mulliken (½(IE+EA)), Allred–Rochow (force via Z_eff/r²).
- **Relativistic effects:** Au color/electronegativity, Hg "noble-gas-like" liquid, Tl/Pb **inert-pair effect**
  (Tl³⁺ IE 5438 vs In³⁺ 5082 kJ/mol).
