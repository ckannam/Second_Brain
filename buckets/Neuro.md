# Neuro  (bucket / MOC)

Cole's **[[neuro-channel|Neuro]]** venture — the YouTube channel where a walking-brain stick
figure explains neuroscience in ~45–90s Shorts, to make brain science accessible. The creator/
output side of his [[neuroscience-of-behavior|neuroscience]] work.

## The venture
- **[[neuro-channel]]** — hub: mission, character, pipeline, status (video #1 rendered), tech, and

- **[[neuro-free-tool-stack]]** — the $0 / no-new-subscription (Claude-only) tool stack + review-before-post gate.
