Introducing Claude Science, a new research app from Anthropic now in public beta.  
  
It runs analyses, searches databases, and traces steps from data wrangling to validation. Every artifact ships with the exact code, environment, and conversation that produced it. It’s all reproducible months later, by anyone on your team.  
  
Runs on your laptop, your cluster, or GPUs on demand. Pre-configured for genomics, single-cell, proteomics, structural biology, and cheminformatics, and can be connected to 60+ scientific databases from day one.  
  
Available on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans for macOS and Linux.  
  
Learn more: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench  
Download Claude Science: http://claude.com/science
