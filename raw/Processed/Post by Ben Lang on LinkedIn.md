The best roles at breakout startups often get filled before they're ever posted.

Here's how to find those roles before everyone else:

1) Ramp's monthly vendor reports Ramp tracks where fast-growing companies spend money. Their reports surface the teams scaling fastest, often before everyone else. Good opportunity to reach out!

2) Harmonic's quarterly Hot 25 A ranked list of the fastest-growing startups, built on real growth data. Ping the teams that have been featured.

3) VC portfolio job boards Sequoia, a16z, Index, Greylock. Most VC funds list jobs at their portfolio companies. Often times one of the best way to discover startup jobs.

4) Founders You Should Know FYSW is highly selective in the fast growing startups they profile. One of the best signals for discovering companies worth pursuing.

5) Next Play newsletter Weekly digest of under-the-radar startups with roles you may not have come across before.

6) YC startup directory Every YC company, searchable by batch, industry, and stage, with relevant job postings for each company. YC founders will reach out to candidates directly.

7) Early Days Substack Deep dives and job listings at companies you should take a closer look at.

8) a16z Build newsletter Weekly newsletter of startup roles curated from Twitter and other undiscovered sources.

9) HN "Who's Hiring" threads Hacker News threads are posted once a month. Recruiters are not allowed to post. Great source.

Links to all 9 sources will be in the comments.
