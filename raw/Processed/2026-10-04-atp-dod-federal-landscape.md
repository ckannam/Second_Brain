### TL;DR / recommended near-term moves
1. Submit to xTech|Search 10 by Oct 19, 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET — framed as Soldier readiness, not medicine.
2. Request a seat at the USSOCOM/SOFWERX small business networking event by Oct 8, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET.
3. Start building a relationship with AFRL 711th Human Performance Wing now, via the AFRL Small
   Business Office and the wing's always-open CHEERS announcement.
None of the DoD SBIR/STTR topics open right now (FY26 Release 6, closing Oct 21) is a clean ATP
match. The real SBIR target is the monthly FY2027 releases, pre-releasing Oct 7, 2026.

### Key findings
- **SBIR/STTR is back on a monthly calendar.** Authority expired Sep 30, 2025; reauthorized Apr 13,
  2026 (S.3971) through Sep 30, 2031. New Phase II "strategic breakthrough" vehicle ($30M ceiling,
  DoD needs POM commitment + ≥20% new non-SBIR DoD matching); more foreign-risk/supply-chain
  screening; annual per-firm Phase I caps; FY26→FY27 carryover. DoD ("Department of War") runs
  monthly releases on DSIP (dodsbirsttr.mil). FY27 Release 1: pre-release Oct 7, opens Oct 28,
  closes Nov 25, 2026.
- **xTech|Search 10** — Army open-topic prize comp (10 U.S.C. §4025) → Army SBIR (ARM26BX05-NP011)
  or STTR (ARM26TX05-NP002) Phase I. Window Sep 10 – Oct 19, 2026, 5pm ET (3-page white paper via
  Valid Eval, NOT DSIP). R1 ≤50 semifinalists $5K each; R2 virtual pitch Jan 18–29, 2027, ≤20
  finalists $20K each; R3 finals Mar 2–4, 2027 (eMerge Americas, Miami) $200K/$100K/$50K; R4 Mar 4–
  Apr 30, 2027 Army SBIR/STTR Phase I up to $300K. Up to $1M cash + up to $6M SBIR. Eligibility:
  for-profit US small business, >50% US-owned, ≤500 employees, one submission/company. Exclusion:
  tech falling *exclusively* in Army Medical Research & Development Command portfolio (incl. military
  operational medicine) is out → ATP must pitch as unit readiness/training-load for commanders, not a
  clinical device. Must disclose the Navy SBIR ("not substantially the same"). Reported scoring
  (secondary): Technical 40 / Army Benefits 25 / Commercial 25 / Intro 5 / Quality 5.
- **711th Human Performance Wing** — Human Effectiveness Directorate + USAF School of Aerospace
  Medicine. Lists wearables as a featured success area ("real-time physical and cognitive insights…
  enhance warfighter readiness, inform personalized interventions (medicine, nutrition, training)").
  CHEERS Multiple Authority Announcement (umbrella FA2384-24-S-2233; Open Period 1 BAA
  FA2384-24-S-2334; Open Period 2 experimental procurement) accepts 5-page white papers anytime
  until Sep 30, 2027, 5pm ET. DoDAACs FA2384 & FA2396; top NAICS 541715, 541990, 561210, 541713,
  334516. Barbara Knight = AFRL Small Business Office Acquisition Manager
  (barbara.knight.1@us.af.mil). Office inbox afrl.sb.questions@us.af.mil. Other contacts: Director
  Brian McJilton (brian.mcjilton@us.af.mil), Deputy Shanel Fultz, Tech Scout Terry Cunningham II,
  Wright Research Site SB Professional Tara Norris (tara.norris@us.af.mil). Older ARMR BAA
  (FA8650-20-S-6008) closed Apr 30, 2026.
- **AFWERX** (DAF SBIR/STTR, TACFI/STRATFI). Replaced "propose anything" Open Topic with Focused
  Open Topics (mission-named gap, solution open); plans to take part in all 6 FY releases. Open now:
  DoW SBIR 26.BZ/26.BX & STTR 26.TZ/26.TX Release 6, close Oct 21, 2026 — counter-drone/space/RF/
  quantum, no human-performance topic. TACFI $375K–$2M; STRATFI $3M–$15M (≤48 mo); one STRATFI + one
  TACFI per company lifetime; only a gov PoC can submit; Phase II projects only; applies ONLY to DAF
  SBIR — ATP's Navy Phase II does NOT qualify. CMMC L1/L2 self-assessment required until Nov 10, 2026.
- **SOCOM / POTFF** — Preservation of the Force & Family, 5 domains; physical domain = "Sports
  Medicine, Strength & Conditioning, Performance Nutrition," goal "early access to care to reduce
  illness/injury duration/severity." Current support contract POTFF II (KBR Wyle) reported $955M /
  791 people, ends Oct 22, 2026 (figure unverified; original 2018 Wyle award H92400-19-D-0001 had a
  $500M ceiling). POTFF III follow-on reported $1.5B ceiling, ~Dec 2026 award (secondary source,
  unverified). ATP path = subcontract/team with the winning prime + Engage SOF, not a direct prime.
- **SOFWERX** — 501(c)(3) innovation platform in Tampa for USSOCOM. OSBP Networking Event ("speed
  dating" w/ gov stakeholders, primes, investors): Nov 5, 2026 at SOFWERX Tampa; request to attend by
  Oct 8, 2026, 11:59pm ET; virtual Q&A Oct 22; confirm via networking-app profile by Oct 29. Tours
  every Wed 9am & 4pm. Tech Tuesday: paused to new submissions since Sep 18 ("no set date" to reopen)
  — fill the "get notified" form.
- **SOCOM / SOF AT&L (Connect With Us)** — Engage SOF on Vulcan (open continuous capability
  submission, reviewed + referred to program offices); Technical Experimentation events; SBIR/STTR;
  BAAs (S&T BAA valid to Dec 31, 2030; Biomedical BAA via US Army); CRADAs (non-financial work plans,
  discovery@socom.mil); OSBP ("one of a small business' first contacts"). CRADA = realistic early step
  (SOF data/users without funding).
- **Musculoskeletal injury** = DoD's top readiness problem. MTEC FY26 Multi-Topic RPP
  (MTEC-26-04-MultiTopic, issued Aug 6, 2026, due Oct 30, 2026): Focus Area 10 pre-hospital MSK
  prototypes — 10A accelerating recovery (~$3M each, 1–2 awards), 10B preserving MSK health (~$1.5M
  each, 2–3 awards), both ≤36 mo; FA2 total force fitness in National Guard = secondary angle.
  Requires MTEC membership, TRL≥3, preliminary data, "Enhanced White Paper." CDMRP/PRMRP (USAMRDC):
  annual, FY26 $370M incl. MSK health; FY26 deadlines passed (Discovery LOI Jul 16 / app Jul 30;
  Clinical Trial apps Sep 22). FY27 ~spring 2027. Most PRMRP awards suit an academic lead (JHTV role).
- **ARL / Army SBIR** — DEVCOM ARL BAA for Foundational Research (W911NF-23-S-0001), combined
  contracts+grants BAA, any-size business, open through Nov 20, 2027. Preliminary inquiry with topic
  POC recommended. STIR awards up to $60K / 9 mo (unverified). Open Campus status unconfirmed. Army
  topics now in monthly DSIP + ad-hoc ASA(ALT) releases (e.g. xTech). Sign up at sbir.army.mil.
- **Other** — DoD SBIR/STTR FY26 Release 6 closes Oct 21, 2026, 12pm ET (closest topics are DARPA
  casualty-care, not readiness — DPA26BZ06-DV025 occult hemorrhage, DPA26BZ06-DV027 CORPS). DHA SBIR
  posts within DSIP (past fits: DHA26BZ01-NV004 wearable biomarker device 2026; wearable metabolic
  sensor 2024); no current deadline. DIU Human Systems covers wearables/human performance, OT in
  60–90 days, but 0 open CSOs now. Navy follow-on: NAVAIR Phase II ~$1.3M (base ≤$1.0M, +option
  ≤$1.3M, 24–36 mo); any agency can award Phase III non-competitively with non-SBIR funds; Navy SBIR
  Transition Program (Navy STP) free 11-mo program for Navy Phase II awardees ("68% greater likelihood
  of transitioning"); Navy Launch commercialization program. Earlier 2026 topics worth watching for
  reposts: DARPA DPA26BZ03-DV012 "Engineering Sleep for Cognitive Performance"; Navy DON26BZ05-NV068
  "Intelligent Tools for Naval Aircrew Performance and Readiness."
- **Context to cite in pitches** — 2023 health.mil report on fitness wearables (reducing MSK injuries
  as a target capability); USU's MIRROR program (predicting MSK injury + return to duty); NC's NC-COMP
  consortium (>80 orgs, led by RTI) builds human-performance tech for DoD.
