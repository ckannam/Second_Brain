## What Is Spaced Repetition and How Does It Work?

Spaced repetition is a learning technique that involves reviewing information at increasing intervals. Instead of cramming, you review material just as you are about to forget it. This method leverages the psychological spacing effect to maximize long-term retention.[Learn about spaced repetition and active recall.](https://spacerep.app/blog/active-recall)

## Why Use Spaced Repetition for Study?

Traditional study methods like re-reading or distinct massed practice like [cramming](https://spacerep.app/blog/cramming-fails) are inefficient. Spaced repetition is the most effective way to transfer knowledge from short-term to long-term memory. It saves time by ensuring you only study what you don't know well.

### Does spaced repetition actually work?

Yes. Spaced repetition is backed by over 100 years of psychology research, starting with Hermann Ebbinghaus. It is scientifically proven to be the most efficient way to encode long-term memories.
