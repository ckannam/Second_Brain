## Endorsement — how it works
- "Endorsement" = NCS uploads an evaluation form highlighting your strengths to national reviewers.
- **Not competitive / not capped** — Duke endorses ALL Duke-affiliated applicants who apply; they want to.
- Working with NCS is optional — you can apply directly; they tick "NCS didn't work with this applicant" and it moves forward, no penalty.
- To get endorsed: use the **"Lock application"** tab in the national portal to mark it ready for campus review. **Do NOT hit "submit"** until ready for the final national submission.
- Meeting the campus deadline doesn't *guarantee* endorsement, but they want to; if serious concerns, they'll contact you directly with suggestions.
